# From the Editor

There is a pattern that keeps repeating.

When an AI deployment struggles, the first question is often, *What is wrong with the model?*

Sometimes the answer is the model.

More often, it is not.

Over the past several years, I have spent time observing organizations, technologies, workflows, and the increasingly complex systems that connect them. Again and again, I found that the greatest obstacles to successful AI deployment were already present before AI arrived.

Fragmented processes, unclear ownership, competing sources of truth, disconnected systems, and operational drift do not disappear when intelligence is added.

They become more visible.

That observation became the starting point for this publication.

**unDrifted** exists to explore the relationship between systems, governance, and artificial intelligence. Not through speculation alone, but through operational examples, research questions, and practical methods for measuring the environments AI depends on.

Every issue will ask difficult questions.

Some articles will examine current events.

Some will explore theory.

Others will document the development of Measures Registry itself as an evolving reference implementation.

Together, they form an ongoing investigation into a larger question:

> What would it take to build systems that are genuinely governable?

The first issue begins with what has become the central observation guiding this work:

**AI isn't broken. Systems are.**

That statement is not intended as criticism.

It is an invitation to look in a different direction.

If we want trustworthy AI, we have to pay as much attention to the environments surrounding intelligence as we do to intelligence itself.

Thank you for taking the time to read the first issue.

I hope it challenges some assumptions, raises better questions, and encourages you to look at your own environment with fresh eyes.

Because the future of AI will not be shaped by models alone.

It will be shaped by the systems we choose to build around them.

Welcome to **Issue 001**.

Let's begin.

**Stephanie Joanne Gaffney**
*Founder & Systems Designer, Measures Registry*
*Editor, unDrifted*

## Acknowledgment

This publication was developed through a sustained collaboration between human direction and artificial intelligence.

Every article, system, and implementation reflects human responsibility for the ideas presented while recognizing the growing role AI can play as a research partner, systems advisor, and implementation collaborator.

Measures Registry itself is an exploration of that relationship.

The work presented here would not exist in this form without that collaboration. Human judgment remains responsible for the direction, claims, and decisions. AI assistance helped surface patterns, test language, structure ideas, challenge drift, and accelerate implementation inside a governed process.

That distinction matters.

Building with AI is not the same as delegating to AI.

The work is strongest when human responsibility and AI capability remain in right relation.
