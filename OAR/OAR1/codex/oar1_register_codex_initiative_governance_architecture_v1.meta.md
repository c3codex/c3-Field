# OAR1: Register Codex Initiative Governance Architecture

## Summary

Registration closeout, per OAR2 constraints. **No implementation, schema, runtime, database migration, or
activation was performed.** This document formally records the Codex Initiative Governance Architecture as
recognized architectural authority, filed as a new `codex` category alongside the existing
`{c3_field, commerce, infrastructure, launch, publication, research}` OAR taxonomy — a category the source OAR2
itself calls for by explicitly disclaiming Measures Registry and c3 Field ownership of these concepts.

No prior document in this repo defined a `c3 Model`, a Standing lifecycle, or an explicit Codex/c3-Field/
registered-systems authority layering. This registration is the first formal record of all three. It is
consistent with, and extends, the existing `docs/_source/codex/dao_codex_declaration.meta.md`, which already
established "Codex holds authority. Field structures relation. Measures registers sequence, access, and state"
at the legal-entity level — this OAR1 is the corresponding architectural-governance-level record.

## 1. Authority Layering (Determination 1) — REGISTERED

Codex is recorded as the governance authority for modeled initiatives. The c3 Field operates. Registered
systems execute their own institutional missions. This layering is now the reference authority for all future
executor advisement — see §4.

## 2. Registration-Precedes-Operation (Determination 2) — REGISTERED

No initiative acquires operational standing without Codex registration. Recorded as binding for all future
initiative work.

## 3. The c3 Model (Determination 3) — REGISTERED

Recorded as the initiative governance model, determining initiative standing:

**c1 Connect → c2 Contribute → Standing Review → c3 Create → Operational Standing**

This is registered as stated in the OAR2, verbatim. No stage definitions, entry/exit criteria, or governing
mechanics beyond what the OAR2 specified were added or inferred by this closeout — those remain for future
governance work, not this registration.

## 4. Executor Advisement as a c2 Contribute Function (Determination 4) — REGISTERED

Executor advisement (the role performed in
`OAR/OAR1/research/oar1_executor_architecture_analysis_measures_registry_undrifted_editorial_research_initiative_v1.meta.md`)
is recorded as a governance function of **c2 Contribute**. It evaluates implementation readiness; it does not
determine initiative standing. Standing remains a Codex governance determination. This resolves the limitation
the prior advisory itself surfaced: it had no higher authority to evaluate against besides Measures Registry
architecture. That advisory's findings remain valid as a **c2 Contribute** readiness evaluation; they are not
retroactively converted into a standing determination by this registration.

## 5. Standing Lifecycle (Determination 5) — REGISTERED

Recorded lifecycle: **Proposed → Under Review → Standing Granted → Registered → Operational → Held →
Retired.** Recorded as suggested per the OAR2's own qualification ("Suggested lifecycle") — not yet declared
mandatory/final by this closeout.

## 6. Measures Registry Autonomy (Determination 6) — REGISTERED, NO CHANGE TO CURRENT STATE

Measures Registry remains autonomous and does not own initiative governance; it consumes modeled initiatives
after standing is granted through Codex governance. No existing Measures Registry authority, route, table, or
process was altered by this registration — confirmed by inspection, this OAR made no code/schema/runtime
changes.

## 7. unDrifted Role Expansion — Recognized, Not Operational (Determination 7) — REGISTERED

unDrifted's expanded role (editorial publication, evidence observation, research coordination, institutional
learning support, and support for MAP / Foundations Educational Modules / SEAT / Computational Systems
Governance) is recorded as **recognized**, not operational. Per Determination 9 (§9 below), none of the
research-coordination or institutional-learning portions of this expanded role may proceed operationally until
contributor governance architecture is seated. This support informs future institutional review; it does not
modify institutional authority.

## 8. Publication/Research Domain Separation (Determination 8) — REGISTERED

Publication architecture (distributes institutional knowledge) and research architecture (develops
institutional knowledge) are recorded as distinct domains. Future implementation — including any Phase 1–4
sequencing from the prior executor advisory — shall preserve this separation rather than merge publication and
research schema/workflow into one undifferentiated system.

## 9. Contributor Governance Prerequisite (Determination 9) — REGISTERED, BINDING GATE

Contributor governance (editorial review, research contribution, evidence review, role governance,
institutional participation) is recorded as a prerequisite architecture. None of these five functions shall
proceed until contributor governance architecture is seated. This formalizes, at Codex authority level, the
gap the prior advisory identified as self-acknowledged-but-unbuilt (`Assets/Registry/asset_registry.md`'s
"Role Workbench" reference) and named as Phase 2 in that advisory's integration sequence. It is now a
registered gate, not merely an advisory recommendation.

## Governance Outcome

Architectural authority only. No implementation, schema, runtime, database migration, activation, or c3 Field
operational standing was granted or performed by this OAR.

## Future Work

Future initiatives register through Codex modeled initiative governance under this architecture. Measures
Registry / unDrifted is designated to become the first modeled initiative registered under this governance
architecture, pending separate approval — not granted by this closeout.

## Expected Result — Confirmed

Future executor advisement now has an explicit authority to evaluate against (Codex governance architecture,
this document) rather than Measures Registry architecture alone. Governance precedes implementation. Standing
precedes operation.
