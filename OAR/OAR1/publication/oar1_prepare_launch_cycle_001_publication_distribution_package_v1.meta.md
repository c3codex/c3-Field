# OAR1: Launch Cycle 001 Publication Distribution Package

Complete Publication Distribution Package prepared at
`docs/_source/codex/publications/launch_cycle_001_publication_distribution_package_v1.meta.md`. Every
deliverable required by the authorizing OAR2 was produced. **Neither canonical article was modified.** No
publication, scheduling, or credential action was taken — the package is drafted content awaiting explicit
operator authorization, per the OAR2's own Authority Boundary.

## 1. Direct Responses

Prepared for both publications × three platforms (Paragraph, X, Facebook) — six total, framed as introductions
(a framing line for Paragraph preceding the full canonical text; a thread-starter for X; a slightly longer
conversational post for Facebook), not summaries or rewrites of either article.

## 2. Editorial Derivatives

Prepared per publication: 5 quotation selections each (all verbatim from canonical text), 2–3 publication
excerpts each (named sections, not paraphrased), 3 discussion prompts each, 1 editorial pull quote each, short
teaser copy, and publication preview copy. All content traces directly to the approved, independently-verified
article text — no new factual claim was introduced.

## 3. Visual Derivatives

Recommendations only, as scoped: square social graphic, landscape header, story format, quote card layout, and
banner usage — each built from the two already-registered Editorial Identity Assets, no redesign proposed.
Actual image production was explicitly not authorized ("no redesign is authorized," and no image-composition
tool was available this pass) and was not attempted.

## 4. Media Derivatives

Reviewed existing governed video assets against the OAR2's four target formats. Finding: `ai_isnt_broken_intro.mp4`
is a strong thematic fit for the Response article (shares its closing line and framing) but predates this
cycle and doesn't mention agents specifically; `assessment_report_orientation.mp4` is a weaker, product-specific
fit; the Issue Promotion reel script has no corresponding video asset at all. **No existing asset cleanly fits
a purpose-made Reel or Facebook video for this specific cycle** — recommended this be either skipped for this
release or treated as its own future production request, since new media generation was explicitly out of
scope here.

## 5. Publication Sequence

Prepared with rationale added to each of the OAR2's six named steps (Paragraph → Website → X → Facebook →
Instagram derivatives → YouTube references), explaining why each ordering choice follows from what precedes it.
Recorded as advisory only, per the OAR2's own framing — no publication authorization was granted or implied by
sequencing it.

## 6. Institutional Response

Drafted a Measures Registry institutional acknowledgement, written to speak as the institution rather than in
unDrifted's editorial voice, explicitly stating it does not itself constitute a methodology change, assessment
finding, or institutional position on any organization named in either publication — preserving the
editorial/institutional distinction the OAR2 required.
