## Verification Performed Before Writing Anything

Before consolidating, one direct contradiction was investigated rather than assumed either way: the OAR2 asked
this review to include five "already public" YouTube video IDs, while every prior verified record in this
session (Buffer execution evidence, the direct-YouTube-OAuth OAR1, this executor's own investigation) showed
YouTube publication as either drafted, rejected, or blocked on missing credentials — never published. All five
video IDs were checked directly against YouTube's oembed endpoint. **All five are real, live, and confirmed on
the actual Measures Registry channel**, with real titles cleanly mapping to four of the five canonical assets
(the fifth, `crystal_seat_orientation.mp4`, by elimination). This was published through a path no OAR in this
repo documents — most plausibly a manual YouTube Studio upload — and is recorded as a genuine, verified finding,
not an assumption either that the claim was true or that it was fabricated.

## Honesty on Incomplete Prior Work

This review does not present the video-derivative production work as complete. Direct verification found: the
intro's vertical+captioned render is corrupted (`moov atom not found`, never re-rendered after the Buffer
investigation interrupted it); the assessment video's primary and alternate short-form clips were planned
(captions hand-built and timing-verified) but never actually rendered; the Phase 2 editorial inspection
(obsidian/crystal/about) never progressed past raw technical inventory — frame stills were extracted but never
reviewed. This is reported as incomplete, not rounded up to "done," per the OAR2's own explicit prohibition on
claiming an asset ready without verifying it.
