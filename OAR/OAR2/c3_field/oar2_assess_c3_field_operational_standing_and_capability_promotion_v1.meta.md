# OBJECTIVE

Conduct a comprehensive assessment of c3 Field based on demonstrated implementation.

Do not expand c3 Field.

Do not create new capabilities.

Do not migrate existing Measures Registry capabilities.

Determine what has been proven, what remains institution-specific, and what has earned promotion into c3 Field.

Measures Registry Phase I operational standing serves as the evidence base for this assessment.

## OBSERVED

Measures Registry has demonstrated operational capability across:

- Registry-driven architecture
- OAR lifecycle
- Publication
- Campaigns
- Commerce
- Stripe integration
- Distribution
- Evidence recording
- Asset registration
- Human / AI execution boundaries

Throughout implementation, several capabilities emerged that appear to belong to c3 Field rather than Measures Registry.

No formal capability inventory currently exists.

## ALIGNED

c3 Field owns capabilities.

Institutions own implementations.

Capabilities are promoted only after operational proof.

Nothing is promoted because it is merely useful.

Promotion requires demonstrated reuse potential.

## ROUTED

### 1. Inventory current c3 Field

Produce a complete inventory of current Field capabilities.

For each capability identify:

- purpose
- current implementation
- maturity
- dependencies
- evidence of operation
- current owner

Return gaps and overlaps.

### 2. Inventory Measures Registry

Identify every significant subsystem now operating inside Measures Registry.

Group by capability rather than directory.

Examples include:

- publication
- campaign
- distribution
- commerce
- renderer
- evidence
- registry
- asset management
- identity
- chamber architecture

Do not assume ownership.

### 3. Capability Promotion Assessment

For every subsystem determine one of:

PROMOTE

HOLD

INSTITUTION ONLY

Promotion requires all of:

- operationally demonstrated
- institution-independent
- reusable
- authority preserving
- suitable for multiple institutions

Return rationale for every decision.

### 4. Architecture Boundary Review

Produce a Field boundary describing:

c3 Field responsibilities

Measures Registry responsibilities

Future institution responsibilities

Highlight any current drift.

Recommend corrections only.

Do not implement corrections.

### 5. Operational Standing

Assess current standing of c3 Field itself.

Suggested maturity classifications:

- conceptual
- architectural
- implemented
- operational
- reusable
- scalable

Support each conclusion with evidence.

### 6. Future Capability Candidates

Identify capabilities that appear ready for future Field promotion.

Examples may include:

- Publication Surface
- Distribution Execution Registry
- Metadata Renderer
- Asset Registry
- Identity
- Commerce
- c3 Ledger

Do not promote automatically.

Recommend:

ready

emerging

held

with rationale.

### 7. Deliverables

Return:

1. c3 Field capability inventory

2. Measures Registry capability inventory

3. promotion matrix

4. architecture boundary map

5. maturity assessment

6. recommended promotion roadmap

7. held concepts

8. identified drift

9. recommended next architectural sequence

## VALIDATION

Return OAR1 with:

- complete inventories
- promotion decisions
- evidence supporting each decision
- architecture diagrams where appropriate
- no implementation changes
- no migrations
- no schema modifications
- no runtime modifications

Future architectural work can proceed from demonstrated capability rather than assumption.

No capabilities are promoted without evidence.
