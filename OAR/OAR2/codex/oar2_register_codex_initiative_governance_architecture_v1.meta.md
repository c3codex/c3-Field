# OBJECTIVE

Following executor advisement, register the architectural governance concepts developed through Codex
discussion.

This OAR does NOT authorize implementation. Its purpose is to formally recognize architectural authority
before future implementation work proceeds.

# BACKGROUND

Executor advisement confirmed that the current Measures Registry architecture is capable of supporting
editorial publication but that research coordination, contributor governance, institutional learning, and
source registration remain architectural gaps.

The advisory also demonstrated an important architectural limitation: the executor was required to evaluate
the initiative from within the Measures Registry architecture because no higher governance authority had yet
been formally registered.

Subsequent Codex review determined that these governance concepts do not belong to Measures Registry, nor do
they currently belong to the operational c3 Field. They belong to the Codex.

# ARCHITECTURAL DETERMINATIONS

1. **Authority layering.** Codex is the governance authority for modeled initiatives. The Codex governs. The
   c3 Field operates. Registered systems execute their own institutional missions.

2. **Registration precedes operation.** Every initiative shall first become a modeled Codex initiative before
   operational work begins. No initiative acquires operational standing without Codex registration.

3. **The c3 Model** is recognized as the initiative governance model. Its purpose is to determine initiative
   standing: c1 Connect → c2 Contribute → Standing Review → c3 Create → Operational Standing.

4. **Executor Advisement** is recognized as a governance function of c2 Contribute. Executor advisement
   evaluates implementation readiness. It does not determine initiative standing — standing remains a
   governance determination.

5. **Standing lifecycle.** Standing is recognized as the initiative lifecycle. Suggested lifecycle: Proposed →
   Under Review → Standing Granted → Registered → Operational → Held → Retired.

6. **Measures Registry autonomy preserved.** Measures Registry remains autonomous. Measures Registry does not
   own initiative governance. Measures Registry consumes modeled initiatives after standing has been granted
   through Codex governance.

7. **unDrifted role expansion (recognized, not yet operational).** unDrifted remains an autonomous Measures
   Registry initiative. Its role is expanded to: editorial publication, evidence observation, research
   coordination, institutional learning support, and support for future development of MAP, Foundations
   Educational Modules, SEAT, and Computational Systems Governance. This support informs future institutional
   review; it does not modify institutional authority.

8. **Domain separation.** Publication architecture and research architecture are recognized as distinct
   architectural domains. Publication distributes institutional knowledge. Research develops institutional
   knowledge. Future implementation shall preserve this separation.

9. **Contributor governance prerequisite.** Contributor governance is recognized as a prerequisite
   architecture. Editorial review, research contribution, evidence review, role governance, and institutional
   participation shall not proceed until contributor governance architecture is seated.

# GOVERNANCE OUTCOME

This OAR establishes architectural authority only. No implementation. No schema. No runtime. No database
migration. No activation. No c3 Field operational standing.

# FUTURE WORK

Future initiatives shall register through Codex modeled initiative governance. Measures Registry / unDrifted
shall become the first modeled initiative registered under this governance architecture following approval.

# EXPECTED RESULT

Future executor advisement evaluates initiatives against Codex governance architecture rather than Measures
Registry architecture alone. Architectural authority becomes explicit. Governance precedes implementation.
Standing precedes operation.
