# OAR2: Produce Launch Cycle 001 Publication Asset Package

## Objective

Produce the actual approval-ready visual and media assets required for Launch Cycle 001
distribution. The editorial inspection phase is complete. This OAR advances the selected source
frames and existing approved media into finished publication assets. Claude shall now produce
deliverables rather than further inspection reports. This OAR does not authorize publication,
scheduling, Buffer execution, or correspondence delivery.

## Governing Boundary

Claude owns creative production. Cody owns publication operations. The Operator retains approval
authority.

```text
Registered Source
→ Editorial Selection
→ Finished Publication Asset
→ Operator Approval
→ Cody Buffer Execution
→ Publication Evidence
```

Do not perform Buffer routing, scheduling, endpoint execution, OAuth work, or publication
evidence updates.

## Approved Source Selections

Governed by `launch_cycle_001_phase2_editorial_stills_inspection_v1.meta.md`: Obsidian `f07`
(hero/thumbnail) and `f04` (dramatic); Crystal `f06` (presenter reference, superseded by a fresh
caption-free extraction per this OAR2's required action); About `f09` (quote card) and `f05`
(explainer card).

## Required Visual Deliverables

1. Field Findings publication assets: landscape, square, vertical story, quote card,
   discussion-prompt card — using the registered banner and approved copy, brand preserved.
2. unDrifted Response publication assets: same five types, using the registered Response banner
   and approved copy, unDrifted Response identity preserved, no Codex/Codexstone references.
3. Measures Registry evergreen assets: Obsidian thumbnail and dramatic still, a clean Crystal
   Seat presenter thumbnail from a fresh uncaptioned extraction, About quote card and explainer
   card.

## Video Derivative Completion

Re-render the corrupted AI Isn't Broken 9:16 captioned derivative; render the planned Assessment
Report primary and alternate clips with verified captions; complete a bounded primary derivative
pass for Obsidian, Crystal, and About where a coherent clip exists, without inflating volume for
its own sake.

## Platform Output Requirements

Social square 1080×1080; landscape 1920×1080 or proportional equivalent; vertical story/reel
1080×1920. Text inside safe areas.

## Typography and Identity

Use established Measures Registry / unDrifted visual language. Restrained, legible, institutional,
high contrast. No new brand system, no generic SaaS styling, no clickbait language. Field
Findings and Response assets visibly related but clearly distinct.

## Copy Use

Approved canonical article text, quotations, teaser copy, and discussion prompts only. No new
factual claims. Shortened quotes preserve meaning; source marked in sidecar metadata.

## Sidecar Metadata / Operator Approval Package

Every finished asset carries sidecar metadata (asset ID, title, source, parent asset, class,
exact copy, dimensions, format, intended endpoints, publication relationship, approval status,
created date, executor, rendered file location, checksum, notes) and is indexed in one Operator
Approval Package grouped by Field Findings, unDrifted Response, Measures Registry evergreen, and
video derivatives.

## Constraints

Do not publish, schedule, access Buffer credentials, perform Cody's operational work, modify
canonical articles or source videos, redesign registered publication identities, invent new
claims, or classify incomplete renders as ready.

HELD, or HELD WITH REASON.
