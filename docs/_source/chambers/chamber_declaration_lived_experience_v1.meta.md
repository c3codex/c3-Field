# Chamber Declaration — Lived Experience

## Purpose

Define how Lived Experience enters system without:

- collapsing into narrative authority
- bypassing Codex verification
- leaking into unstructured Field placement
- becoming Legacy by assertion alone

## Declaration

Lived Experience is signal carried by a Named Individual through embodiment.

It is:

- valid as signal
- valid for intake
- not valid as authority
- not automatically Legacy

This preserves human expression without collapsing the authority chain. :contentReference[oaicite:11]{index=11} :contentReference[oaicite:12]{index=12}

## Entry Standing

Lived Experience may enter through:

- SRC1 when relation is primary
- SRC2 when contribution-bearing signal is present

It enters through bounded intake, not direct Codex seating. :contentReference[oaicite:13]{index=13}

## Required Carriers

Lived Experience must move through:

- Envelope
- envKey where formed
- OAR1 at first verified touch

This preserves trace and prevents unbounded insertion. :contentReference[oaicite:14]{index=14}

## Conversion Condition

Lived Experience becomes Legacy only if all of the following become true:

- verified through OAR
- structurally placed in Field
- registered in Measures
- validly executed through Chazz
- right-angle alignment resolves

Recognition follows verification, not feeling, intensity, or narrative force. :contentReference[oaicite:15]{index=15} :contentReference[oaicite:16]{index=16} :contentReference[oaicite:17]{index=17}

## Constraints

Lived Experience may not:

- define system truth
- override Codex
- bypass Field relation
- skip Measures registration
- force recognition
- substitute for validation

This is required by role integrity, verification before recognition, and native distinction. :contentReference[oaicite:18]{index=18} :contentReference[oaicite:19]{index=19}

## Failure Handling

If Lived Experience attempts authority or bypass:

1. halt passage
2. preserve OAR trace
3. return to prior valid state
4. require correction before continuance

No narrative intensity counts as system proof.

## Execution Implication

Lived Experience is high-signal input, but untrusted until verified.

This keeps both conditions intact:

- human expression remains receivable
- system integrity remains protected

## Closing

Lived Experience matters.
It does not self-authorize.

Codex holds.
Field structures.
Measures registers.
Chazz executes.
Recognition follows verification.
