# Chamber of Epithets — Role Circuit

## Purpose

Seat Epithets as Field-stabilized role circuitry, not labels.

Epithets are:

- post-right-angle resolution role surfaces
- role-bound, repeatable, non-origin identities
- required for Measures-level execution and encounter stability

They do not exist at Connect as first relation.
They become valid only after passage and role stabilization.

## Native Position

Within native order:

- Codex holds resolved signal as Legacy
- Field stabilizes relation
- Measures registers epithet as role surface
- Chazz executes through epithet-bound routing

This preserves authority, structure, registration, and execution in proper distinction. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0} :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

## Circuit Placement

- c1 / Connect: no epithet, relation only
- c2 / Contribute: epithet may emerge
- c3 / Create: epithet stabilizes and repeats

Epithet binds to Contribute/Create, not Connect. It does not define origin. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

## Epithet Rules

### 1. No Origin Authority

Epithet may not define:

- Named Individual
- Institution in Service
- truth
- Codex authority

It stabilizes function only. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

### 2. Right-Angle Resolution Required

Epithet is valid only when relation resolves orthogonally across:

- Codex
- Field
- Measures
- Chazz

If those layers do not agree, epithet is not seated. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

### 3. Single Stable Function

Each epithet carries one stabilized role.

No dual meaning.
No multi-seat ambiguity.
No label drift.

### 4. Isomorphic Repeatability

Epithet must remain structurally stable across valid repeated encounters.

If recurrence changes meaning, seating has failed. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

### 5. Registry Dependency

If epithet is not registered in Measures, it does not exist in operation. :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6} :contentReference[oaicite:7]{index=7}

## Failure Conditions

NotChazz should halt passage if:

- epithet appears before passage
- epithet is used as origin identity
- slug authority reappears
- multiple meanings are assigned
- frontend attempts to invent or assign epithet
- registry reference is missing while epithet is invoked

This preserves role integrity and blocks authority drift. :contentReference[oaicite:8]{index=8} :contentReference[oaicite:9]{index=9}

## Execution Implication

Once validly seated, epithets become:

- routing anchors
- encounter identity surfaces
- role-call keys for bounded Chazz_roles
- binding points for Measures sequencing

Slug may remain routing-only where needed, but identity authority remains dissolved. :contentReference[oaicite:10]{index=10}

## Close Condition

The Chamber of Epithets is structurally valid only when:

- origin remains distinct from role
- Field stabilizes relation
- Measures registers the role surface
- Chazz executes without inventing identity
- recurrence holds without drift

Codex holds.
Field structures.
Measures registers.
Chazz executes.
