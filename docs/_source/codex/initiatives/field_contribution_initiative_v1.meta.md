# Field Contribution — Modeled Codex Initiative

## Purpose

Establish an ongoing operational practice of contributing governed observations and operational analysis into
the broader Field through unDrifted and Measures Registry.

The objective is contribution. Recognition, if earned, follows contribution.

## Standing

`registered`, per the Standing lifecycle established in `OAR/OAR1/codex/oar1_register_codex_initiative_governance_architecture_v1.meta.md`
(Proposed → Under Review → Standing Granted → **Registered** → Operational → Held → Retired). This is the first
initiative registered under that governance architecture.

Registration establishes the initiative as a recognized Codex object with a name, purpose, and supported
systems. It does not, by itself, constitute a completed operational cycle — see the Launch Cycle 001
operational record (`docs/_source/codex/initiatives/launch_cycle_001_operational_record.meta.md`) for whether
any actual contribution has occurred under this initiative.

## Relationship to Autonomous Systems

Consistent with Determination 6 (Codex Initiative Governance Architecture): Measures Registry remains
autonomous and does not own this initiative; it consumes/supports it. unDrifted remains autonomous within its
registered relationship to Measures Registry. This initiative does not modify either system's authority.

## Governing Determinations Carried Forward

Per the authorizing OAR2:

- Measures Registry remains an autonomous operational methodology.
- unDrifted remains the observational and publication interface.
- Codex remains authority.
- Ledger remains knowledge maturation.
- c3 Field continues toward an operational environment but is not required for this initiative.
- The Field is the shared relational environment in which governed contributions are encountered; c3 Field is
  the governed means of participating in that Field.
