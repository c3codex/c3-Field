# c3 Ledger Entry — c3_ledger_0006

## Originating Inquiry

Can the c2ME.env 3×3 matrix computationally establish relational 3-2-2 completeness even though
Computational Intelligence (CI) has not yet surfaced as a relational actor in that environment?

A second expression of the same inquiry then appeared:

Can a 3×3 matrix also represent the operational positions or roles that must actually be occupied for a
branch to function, for example three Origin Founders, three Foundational Partners, and three Community
Partners?

## Context

The inquiry emerged while defining the c2ME.env boundary, its six Material Environment Touchpoints, its two
CAR expressions, and the Million Dollar Mission participant Ledger Thread.

The Material Environment Qualification Grammar already requires an independently complete Material
Environment to resolve:

- 3 Constraints;
- 2 Agreements;
- 2 Resolutions; and
- 6 Touchpoints.

The six Touchpoints are:

Identity · Ownership · Custody · Verification · Relationship · Persistence.

At the same time, c3 Relational Operations and Systems Governance permits Named Individuals, Institutions in
Service, and Computational Intelligence to participate according to their own nature, role, relationships,
permissions, and standing.

CI has not yet been introduced as a relational actor in the current c2ME.env implementation.

That absence surfaced an important distinction.

## Observed Relations

1. A permitted participant class is not the same thing as a required participant position in every environment.
2. Relational completeness must be evaluated against the positions, conditions, and relations required by the
   environment actually being qualified.
3. A participant class that has not entered a governed relation cannot be treated as a missing relation solely
   because that class exists elsewhere in c3 governance.
4. A new actor entering later should trigger re-evaluation of the relations and touchpoints affected by that
   actor rather than retroactively invalidate a previously complete environment without evidence.
5. The 3×3 matrix can therefore operate as a computational qualification surface if each cell resolves from
   evidence rather than from labels or visual occupancy alone.
6. The six Touchpoints can serve as the evidence dimensions beneath the matrix, making visible whether the
   required relational conditions actually resolve.
7. The same matrix form may also describe required operational positions for a branch if those positions are
   separately governed as requirements rather than inferred from symmetry.
8. The candidate branch-position pattern identified in thread is:
   - 3 Origin Founders;
   - 3 Foundational Partners;
   - 3 Community Partners.
9. These nine positions are not granted standing by this Ledger entry. They are preserved as a candidate
   relational-role expression for branch qualification.
10. A role/position matrix and a Material qualification matrix may be isomorphic without being identical:
    one asks whether required conditions resolve; the other asks whether required responsibilities are
    operationally occupied.

## Discovery

Relational completeness is not the presence of every possible actor.

Relational completeness is the demonstrated resolution of the positions, conditions, and relationships that
the bounded environment or branch actually requires.

A computational 3×3 can therefore function as a stable qualification grammar across changing participants if:

- the required cells are explicitly defined;
- each cell has evidence-backed resolution criteria;
- the six Touchpoints remain independently resolvable;
- absent participant classes are not silently converted into defects;
- newly introduced actors trigger bounded re-evaluation of affected relations;
- and role occupancy is distinguished from participant class.

## Candidate Proposition

A governed environment or branch may be computationally qualified by proving that its required relational
positions and conditions are operational and evidence-backed, without requiring every participant class
permitted by the broader system to be present.

The 3×3 matrix may provide the computational representation of those required positions/conditions when each
cell is governed, independently evidenced, and related to the six Material Environment Touchpoints.

Candidate only. No universal 3×3 role taxonomy, branch-admission rule, CI requirement, or role count is granted
standing by this entry.

## Candidate Branch Role Matrix

The following thread-originated pattern is preserved for testing:

| Position family | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Origin | Origin Founder | Origin Founder | Origin Founder |
| Foundation | Foundational Partner | Foundational Partner | Foundational Partner |
| Community | Community Partner | Community Partner | Community Partner |

This candidate matrix asks a different question from the Material 3-2-2 qualification grammar:

**Are the positions required for this branch operationally occupied and related?**

It must not be interpreted as proof that every branch universally requires these exact nine positions.

Before elevation, branch testing must determine:

- whether all nine positions are actually required;
- whether one person may occupy more than one position;
- whether occupancy is simultaneous or phase-dependent;
- what evidence proves a position is operational;
- whether position vacancy produces HOLD, ROUTE, or partial operation;
- what authority, responsibility, and exit conditions attach to each position;
- and whether the pattern is branch-specific or c3-general.
