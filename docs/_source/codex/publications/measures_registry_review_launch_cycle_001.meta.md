# Measures Registry Review — Launch Cycle 001

## Purpose

Document operational implications for MAP, Foundations Educational Modules, assessment development, and
Computational Systems Governance research, arising from Field Findings 2026-W28 and unDrifted Response 001.

Advisory only. Does not modify Measures Registry authority or methodology.

## MAP Implications

The findings support environmental review of: existing AI and agent inventory; ownership; authority
boundaries; identity; access; dependency mapping; runtime visibility; interruption controls; evidence and
traceability; human approval points. **Recommended disposition: accepted as future evidence input.** Per the
article's own instruction, do not revise MAP assessment criteria solely from this single sweep — this is
source material for future review, not a criteria change.

## Foundations Educational Module Implications

The findings provide contemporary material for modules addressing: the operational environment; why
measurement precedes deployment; systems, assets, and positions; ownership and control; hidden components;
structural drift; AI authority versus AI capability; governance at runtime. **Recommended disposition:
accepted as candidate module material**, pending separate curriculum-design review (not performed here).

## Assessment Implications

No specific assessment-criteria change is identified or recommended. The convergence findings (agent
ownership, authority, identity, and evidence gaps observed across five independent sources) are consistent
with, and reinforce, Measures Registry's existing environmental-assessment premise rather than suggesting a
gap in it. **Recommended disposition: no action** — consistent reinforcement, not a finding requiring
methodology revision.

## Computational Systems Governance Research Implications

The convergence supports research into: governed computational participation; position assignment; human/AI
authority boundaries; relational accountability; operational evidence; the environmental conditions required
for autonomous action. This directly connects to the candidate new inquiry Field Findings itself proposes and
routes to the Ledger review (see below) rather than to this review. **Recommended disposition: accepted as a
live research thread**, tracked via the Ledger's candidate-inquiry mechanism, not as a new Measures Registry
research program on its own authority.

proposes candidate Relational Points of Contact against `c3_ledger_0001` and a candidate new inquiry. Those
