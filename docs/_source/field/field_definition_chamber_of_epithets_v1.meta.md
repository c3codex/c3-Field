# Field Definition — Chamber of Epithets

## Purpose

Define the Chamber of Epithets as the Field surface where role-bearing Epithets are seated for ordered encounter.

## Field Standing

- The Chamber of Epithets is a distinct Field surface
- It is not a gallery and not a grouped abstraction surface
- It holds explicit, named Epithet seats
- It resolves as a 9-seat chamber structured in 3 / 3 / 3

## Function

- seats role-bearing Epithets in explicit order
- provides a stable Field surface for Measures to register encounter
- preserves sequence, distinction, and release integrity

## Structural Conditions

- no grouped placeholder rows
- no implicit or inferred seating
- each seat must resolve to a named Epithet

## Material Conditions

- Chamber permits obsidian, crystal, marble, and lapis
- material is not restricted to oracle-card-only logic
- material reflects role truth, not legacy constraint

## Relation to Other Field Surfaces

- receives passage from Temple and Obsidian surfaces
- receives alignment from Temple and remains structurally distinct from it
- does not replace Temple or Marble Chamber functions

## Boundary

- does not determine release_state
- does not perform registry functions
- does not act as Codex authority

## Closing

The Chamber of Epithets is a living Field surface where roles are seated in order and made available for encounter without collapsing identity, material, or sequence.
