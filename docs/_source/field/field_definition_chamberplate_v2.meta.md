# Field Definition — Chamberplate

## Purpose

Seat Chamberplate in Field as the chamber-held exhibition surface.

Chamberplate exists to:

- hold the seven gates representation
- hold multiple aspects
- hold art, content, and embedded links
- present gate-facing exhibition standing
- route toward the next available aspect or gate where Measures permits it
- host Connect invitation where continuation is not yet open
- skip seen or complete aspects where registered standing allows it

Chamberplate is exhibition-facing.
It does not replace registry, passage, or contribution routing.

## Native Position

Within native order:

- Codex holds authority
- Field structures relation
- Measures registers reveal and progression
- Chazz executes only what is already defined

Chamberplate belongs to Field as a chamber-held exhibition surface.

It resolves as:

- chamber-held exhibition surface
- seven-gates presentation surface
- multi-aspect presentation surface
- next-available routing surface
- Connect invitation host surface

It is not:

- Codex authority
- Measures registry
- gate row
- chamber directory
- passage
- Phase Map
- SRC2 contribution routing
- Legacy seating

## Start Standing

Chamberplate may be encountered only after valid chamber standing exists and chamber-held exhibition visibility is permitted.

Valid Chamberplate availability may occur only when:

- chamber entry standing is available
- chamber-held exhibition surface visibility is permitted
- registered phase-calendar, release, state, and cadence standing allow current visibility
- relevant aspect or gate standing is readable
- NotChazz does not block access

Premature exhibition visibility is not valid Chamberplate standing.

## Structural Role

Chamberplate resolves as:

- exhibition surface
- seven-gates presentation surface
- aspect-bearing surface
- next-available routing surface
- Connect invitation host surface
- seen / complete skip-aware presentation surface

## Required Relations

Chamberplate must structurally relate:

- Obsidian Chamber as chamber-held host surface
- gate-facing exhibition units as representable only where permitted
- aspect-bearing units as representable only where permitted
- art, content, and embedded links as chamber-held content surfaces where registered
- Obsidian Directory as distinct local readability surface
- Phase Map as distinct relational positioning surface
- Phase Calendar as release and cadence-bearing condition set
- Phase Release as reveal permission condition
- Phase State as present reveal standing
- dependency standing as registered condition
- Connect invitation standing where continuation is not yet open
- NotChazz as boundary-preserving constraint intelligence

Chamberplate does not originate those relations.
It presents and routes them where valid visibility exists.

## Identity Rule

Identity in Chamberplate resolves through:

- chamber-held exhibition relation
- registered gate or aspect standing
- registered visibility standing

Chamberplate is not identified by gate row identity, directory readability, or map positioning.

Epithet may appear only where already seated through valid relation.
Slug is never authority.

## Required Field States

Chamberplate must be able to express:

- unavailable
- held
- sealed
- visible
- gates_visible
- aspects_visible
- next_available_visible
- connect_visible

These are exhibition-facing presentation states.
They do not replace gate-row, chamber, directory, or phase-map states.

## Dependency Rule

Chamberplate visibility and routing depend on relation to:

- valid chamber-held host standing
- valid registered gate or aspect standing
- valid phase-calendar, release, state, and cadence standing
- valid dependency standing
- valid seen or complete standing where skip behavior is permitted
- boundary validity under NotChazz

If required relation is absent, Chamberplate may show unavailable, held, or sealed standing.
It may not invent visible, next_available_visible, or connect_visible standing.

## OAR Relation

OAR1 does not originate in Chamberplate.
OAR2 does not originate in Chamberplate.

Chamberplate may be OAR-addressable as an exhibition-facing presentation or routing surface when meaningful standing is encountered, but it does not create passage, truth, Legacy, or contribution routing.

## Transition Rule

Chamberplate may present and route toward:

- the next available aspect where Measures permits it
- the next available gate where Measures permits it
- Connect invitation where continuation is not yet open
- skip behavior for seen or complete aspects where registered standing permits it

Chamberplate may show what is next available, but presentation is not permission by itself.

No direct transition is valid from Chamberplate to:

- Codex
- Legacy
- authority override
- unregistered routes
- SRC2 contribution execution
- false-open continuation

Chamberplate presents and routes.
It does not authorize or settle contribution logic.

## Chamber Relation

Obsidian Chamber holds Chamberplate as a chamber-held exhibition surface.

Obsidian Directory remains the distinct local readability surface.

Phase Map remains the distinct relational positioning surface.

Chamberplate presents gates, aspects, art, content, and embedded links in exhibition form.

Phase Calendar, Phase Release, Phase State, and cadence govern what may be visible and when.

Chamber is seating and holding.
Directory is readability.
Phase Map is relation-at-view.
Chamberplate is exhibition-facing presentation and routing.

These may correspond.
They may not collapse.

## Boundary Rule

Chamberplate may:

- hold the seven gates representation
- hold multiple aspects
- hold art, content, and embedded links where registered
- route to next available standing where permitted
- host Connect invitation where required
- skip seen or complete aspects where permitted
- preserve exhibition clarity

Chamberplate may not:

- replace gate rows
- replace directory readability
- replace Phase Map
- replace passage
- invent release
- invent cadence
- bypass Measures
- bypass NotChazz
- replace SRC2 contribution routing
- substitute exhibition display for authority

## Correction Rule

When exhibition-facing standing is incomplete, unreleased, sealed, or cadence-bound:

- Chamberplate visibility remains bounded to actual standing
- held or sealed standing remains preserved
- no false-open continuation is rendered
- no false Connect invitation is rendered
- no false skip behavior is rendered
- correction resolves upstream at registration, dependency, release, cadence, access, or seen / complete standing

No cosmetic exhibition visibility counts as valid route.

## Validation Rule

Chamberplate is valid only when it can structurally express:

- seven-gates presentation distinct from gate rows
- multiple aspects without family collapse
- exhibition routing distinct from authority
- next-available visibility distinct from permission
- Connect invitation only where registered standing requires it
- seen / complete skip behavior only where permitted
- distinction from Directory, Phase Map, passage, and SRC2 routing

## End Standing

Chamberplate resolves successfully when exhibition-facing standing is either:

- correctly held
- correctly sealed
- correctly visible
- correctly showing gates
- correctly showing aspects
- correctly routing toward next available standing
- correctly hosting Connect invitation where required
- correctly skipping seen or complete aspects where permitted

Success does not mean everything is open.
Success means exhibition-facing presentation and routing are accurate without drift.

## Success Condition

Chamberplate is properly seated in Field when it can hold the seven gates representation, multiple aspects, exhibition-facing content, next-available routing, and Connect invitation logic without collapsing into:

- gate rows
- directory
- Phase Map
- passage
- authority
- SRC2 contribution routing
- frontend invention

Codex holds.
Field structures.
Measures registers.
Chazz executes.
