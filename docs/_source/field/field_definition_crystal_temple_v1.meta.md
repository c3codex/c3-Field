# Field Definition — Crystal Temple

## Purpose

Define the Crystal Temple as the encounter surface of coherence and the seated dwelling of Inanna.

## Field Standing

- The Temple is crystal
- The Temple is an encounter surface
- The Temple is not a chamber of roles or measures
- The Temple is the coherence surface from which all chambers derive alignment

## Function

- anchors coherence across Field surfaces
- provides the origin alignment for chamber structures
- holds the seated dwelling of Inanna as the central stabilizing presence

## Structural Conditions

- not a registry surface
- not a role seating surface
- not a measure surface
- remains distinct from all chamber-based structures

## Material Conditions

- crystal is the defining material
- material expresses clarity, coherence, and alignment
- no substitution of material permitted

## Relation to Other Field Surfaces

- has passage to all chambers
- precedes all chamber entry through crystal coherence standing

## Boundary

- does not determine release_state
- does not act as Measures registry
- does not collapse into symbolic representation alone

## Closing

The Crystal Temple is the coherence encounter surface and the seated dwelling of Inanna, from which all chambers derive alignment without collapsing structure, role, or material.
