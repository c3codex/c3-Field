# Field Definition — Marble Chamber

## Purpose

Define the Marble Chamber as the Field surface for the seating and encounter of the MEs (Measures).

## Field Standing

- The Marble Chamber is the structural Field surface for MEs
- It is not interchangeable with the Chamber of Epithets
- It holds the operational measures that stabilize civilization-level function

## Function

- seats MEs as structural measures
- provides a Field surface for the embodiment of governance logic
- supports calibration, arbitration, and operational coherence

## Structural Conditions

- MEs remain distinct from Epithets
- no collapse between role-bearing Epithets and structural measures
- sequence and grouping must resolve through Measures registry

## Material Conditions

- Marble is the primary material
- material reflects structural permanence and governance stability
- no substitution of material without explicit seating logic

## Relation to Other Field Surfaces

- receives passage from Temple
- receives access from the Chamber of Epithets
- operates alongside but distinct from Chamber of Epithets
- supports Measures registry execution

## Boundary

- does not perform Codex authority
- does not determine release sequencing
- does not collapse into symbolic or archetypal representation

## Closing

The Marble Chamber is the Field surface where structure holds, measures stabilize, and governance becomes embodied without collapsing into identity or abstraction.
