# Field Definition — Obsidian Chamber (Material Entry Surface)

## Purpose

Seat Obsidian in Field as the first material chamber entered after valid passage.

Obsidian is where signal first encounters constraint, reduction, and verification pressure.

It is not intake.
It is not passage.
It is not completion.

It is the first material condition of transformation.

## Field Role

Obsidian resolves as:

- first material chamber
- threshold material environment
- reduction and constraint surface
- encounter-bearing chamber
- post-passage stabilization field

Obsidian does not resolve as:

- intake surface
- passage mechanism
- Codex authority
- Measures registry
- final placement

## Structural Relations

Obsidian must relate to:

- Harrumuk Passage as entry condition
- Antechamber as prior intake state
- OAR1 as attached intake record
- Envelope as carried signal structure
- envKey as continuity access
- NotChazz as constraint enforcement
- Epigraph as first encounter surface within chamber
- Phase Map as post-encounter relational positioning

## Entry Condition

Obsidian may only be entered when:

- Harrumuk Passage resolves
- signal satisfies passage eligibility
- NotChazz permits transition

If passage is not valid:

? Obsidian is not accessible
? signal remains in Antechamber

## Material Function

Obsidian applies:

- constraint
- reduction
- removal of excess signal
- exposure of structural truth

Obsidian does not:

- add meaning
- decorate signal
- amplify signal

It reduces signal to what can survive structure.

## Chamber Behavior

Within Obsidian:

- signal is stabilized under constraint
- structure becomes visible
- incoherence is exposed
- only structurally valid relation persists

Obsidian does not complete signal.
It prepares signal for correct encounter and positioning.

## Encounter Binding

Obsidian is the first chamber that may host encounter.

The first valid encounter within Obsidian is:

? Epigraph

No encounter may render:

- before Obsidian entry
- outside material context
- without passage resolution

## Boundary Rule

Obsidian may:

- hold signal under constraint
- expose structure
- host encounter surfaces

Obsidian may not:

- bypass passage
- grant Codex seating
- simulate completion
- override NotChazz

## Failure State

If signal fails to stabilize within Obsidian:

- signal does not progress
- OAR1 remains attached
- correction path is required
- return or hold may occur

No forward progression without structural integrity.

## Transition Rule

Valid progression from Obsidian is:

Obsidian Chamber ? Epigraph ? Phase Map

Epigraph is encounter.
Phase Map is relational positioning.

## Protection Relation

NotChazz must be able to:

- enforce constraint within Obsidian
- block invalid progression
- prevent bypass of reduction
- preserve structural integrity

## Success Condition

Obsidian is properly seated in Field when:

- it can only be entered through Harrumuk
- it hosts first valid encounter (Epigraph)
- it enforces constraint and reduction
- it prevents premature progression
- it maintains separation from intake and passage

## Closing

Obsidian is where signal is tested by structure.

What survives continues.

What does not is returned.

Codex holds.
Field structures.
Measures registers.
Chazz operates.
NotChazz protects.
