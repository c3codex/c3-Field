# Field Definition — Obsidian Chamber

## Purpose

Seat Obsidian Chamber in Field as the first obsidian material chamber entered after valid threshold crossing.

Obsidian Chamber does not function as passage.
Obsidian Chamber does not function as directory.
Obsidian Chamber does not function as Phase Map.

Obsidian Chamber exists to:

- seat obsidian material standing
- hold chamber-local units in material relation
- contain local release and dependency standing
- hold chamberplate as exhibition surface
- hold Phase Map as chamber-held relational positioning surface
- carry Phase Calendar, phase release, phase state, and cadence standing relevant to exhibition and conversion
- provide the chamber seat within which Obsidian Directory becomes readable
- preserve chamber identity without collapse into visibility logic

## Native Position

Within native order:

- Codex holds authority
- Field structures chamber relation
- Measures registers reveal and progression
- Chazz executes only what is already defined

Obsidian Chamber belongs to Field as a material chamber surface.

It resolves as:

- obsidian material seat
- first material chamber of the obsidian path
- chamber container for local relation
- chamber-held host surface for exhibition structures
- chamber-held host surface for conversion-relevant timing standing
- local host surface for directory readability

It is not:

- Codex authority
- Measures registry
- Harrumuk Passage
- Obsidian Directory
- Phase Map itself
- Chamberplate itself
- Legacy seating

## Start Standing

Obsidian Chamber may be entered only after valid threshold crossing has resolved through Harrumuk Passage.

Valid chamber entry may occur only when:

- one valid exhibition threshold route has resolved
- Harrumuk Passage has completed crossing
- destination chamber standing is available
- Measures-registered chamber relation permits seating
- NotChazz does not block chamber entry

Premature chamber seating is not valid chamber standing.

## Structural Role

Obsidian Chamber resolves as:

- chamber surface
- material seat
- local relation container
- chamber identity surface
- chamber-held host for directory readability
- chamber-held host for chamberplate
- chamber-held host for Phase Map
- chamber-held carrier of Phase Calendar / release / cadence standing
- pre-downstream material standing

## Required Relations

Obsidian Chamber must structurally relate:

- Harrumuk Passage as prior threshold relation
- obsidian material identity as chamber seat
- Measures-registered chamber-local units as contained relation
- release standing as registered local condition
- dependency standing as registered local condition
- Obsidian Directory as chamber-local readability surface
- Chamberplate as chamber-held exhibition surface
- Phase Map as chamber-held relational positioning surface
- Phase Calendar as chamber-relevant release and cadence standing for exhibition and conversion
- Phase Release as reveal permission condition
- Phase State as present reveal standing
- cadence as registered progression condition where applicable
- NotChazz as boundary-preserving constraint intelligence

Obsidian Chamber does not originate those relations.
It seats and holds them in material standing.

## Identity Rule

Identity in Obsidian Chamber resolves through:

- chamber relation
- obsidian material seat
- Measures-registered local standing

Obsidian Chamber is not identified by route string, frontend label, directory visibility, or phase-map visibility.

Epithet may appear only where already seated in valid chamber-local relation.
Slug is never authority.

## Required Field States

Obsidian Chamber must be able to express:

- unavailable
- entered
- seated
- sealed
- active
- held
- local_readability_available
- exhibition_surface_available
- cadence_bound

These are chamber states.
They do not replace directory readability states or phase states.

## Dependency Rule

Chamber seating in Obsidian depends on relation to:

- valid threshold crossing from Harrumuk Passage
- valid chamber registration
- local release standing
- local dependency standing
- valid chamber-held exhibition surface relation where applicable
- valid phase-calendar and cadence standing where applicable
- boundary validity under NotChazz

If required relation is absent, chamber may remain unavailable, held, sealed, or cadence_bound.
It may not invent active standing.

## OAR Relation

OAR1 does not originate in Obsidian Chamber.
OAR2 does not originate in Obsidian Chamber.

Obsidian Chamber may be OAR-addressable as a chamber-standing surface when meaningful entry, holding, release, or cadence-bound standing is encountered, but it does not create truth, passage, or Legacy.

## Transition Rule

Obsidian Chamber may seat valid local standing and host local exhibition and conversion-relevant surfaces.

Obsidian Chamber may host:

- Obsidian Directory for chamber-local readability
- Chamberplate as chamber-held exhibition surface
- Phase Map as chamber-held relational positioning surface
- Phase Calendar standing for release and cadence relevant to exhibition and conversion

Obsidian Chamber may route continuance toward:

- Obsidian Directory for chamber-local readability
- valid chamber-held exhibition surfaces where registered
- valid downstream relation when Measures permits it

No direct transition is valid from Obsidian Chamber to:

- Codex
- Legacy
- authority override
- unregistered downstream units

Chamber seating is not visibility by itself.
Chamber seating is material standing.

## Chamber Relation

Harrumuk Passage resolves crossing into Obsidian Chamber.

Obsidian Chamber resolves material seating after entry.

Obsidian Directory resolves chamber-local readability within the chamber.

Chamberplate and Phase Map are chamber-held exhibition surfaces within the chamber.

Phase Calendar, Phase Release, Phase State, and cadence govern availability and progression relevant to exhibition and conversion.

Passage is movement.
Chamber is seating and holding.
Directory is readability.
Phase Map is relational positioning.
Chamberplate is exhibition surface.

These may correspond.
They may not collapse.

## Boundary Rule

Obsidian Chamber may:

- seat obsidian material standing
- contain registered local relation
- host chamber-local readability through Obsidian Directory
- hold chamberplate and Phase Map as chamber-held surfaces
- carry release and cadence standing relevant to exhibition and conversion
- preserve chamber identity

Obsidian Chamber may not:

- replace passage
- replace directory
- replace Phase Map
- replace Chamberplate
- invent release
- invent cadence
- bypass Measures
- bypass NotChazz
- substitute material seating for visibility logic

## Correction Rule

When chamber relation is incomplete, unreleased, or cadence-bound:

- chamber standing remains bounded to actual state
- sealed, held, or cadence-bound standing remains preserved
- no false chamber activation is rendered
- no false-open exhibition surface is rendered
- correction resolves upstream at threshold, registration, dependency, release, or cadence layer

No cosmetic chamber presence counts as valid standing.

## Validation Rule

Obsidian Chamber is valid only when it can structurally express:

- obsidian material seating distinct from passage
- chamber identity distinct from directory readability
- chamber-held exhibition surfaces distinct from chamber identity
- local release and dependency standing without collapse
- chamber-held Phase Calendar / release / cadence standing without authority drift
- valid chamber-local hosting of readability
- Phase Map as chamber-held surface, not chamber substitute

## End Standing

Obsidian Chamber resolves successfully when chamber standing is either:

- correctly held
- correctly sealed
- correctly seated
- correctly active
- correctly cadence-bound
- correctly hosting valid local readability
- correctly holding valid exhibition surfaces

Success does not mean everything inside the chamber is open.
Success means chamber standing is materially seated without drift.

## Success Condition

Obsidian Chamber is properly seated in Field when it can hold obsidian material relation, chamber-local standing, exhibition surfaces, and registered release/cadence standing without collapsing into:

- passage
- directory
- authority
- Phase Map
- Chamberplate
- frontend invention
