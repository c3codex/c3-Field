# Field Participation Endpoints — Registry of Record

## Governing Determination

Measures Registry remains the governing institutional authority. unDrifted remains the editorial publication
and Field-facing publication system operating under Measures Registry authority. Codex remains the operational
orchestration environment. Each endpoint is registered according to its operational function, not merely its
platform. **No publication authority is created by this registry. No platform is granted governing authority.
Platforms function solely as governed operational endpoints — this registry does not authorize publication or
automate distribution.**

## Verification Note

Endpoints were checked for existence before registration, not assumed from the authorizing OAR2's text alone.
Verification method and result are recorded per-endpoint below, honestly distinguishing confirmed from
inconclusive — two platforms (X, Facebook) structurally block non-authenticated verification (return an empty
JS shell or generic page), which is **not evidence the accounts don't exist**, only that this executor could
not confirm them independently. Recommend the operator confirm those directly.

## Operational Relationships

Measures Registry develops and governs methodology. unDrifted observes, publishes, responds, and participates
within the Field. Codex governs operational execution. Publication distribution derives from approved
publications rather than existing independently — an endpoint's registration here does not itself authorize
anything to be sent through it.
