# MEs ↔ Resolutions Correspondence

## Purpose

Seat the conversion-side relation between MEs and the 7 Resolutions of Coherence without forcing exhibition encounter logic, one-to-one item assignment, or numeric collapse between 7 and 13.

This surface establishes relation, not reveal behavior.
It does not seat encounter defs.
It does not seat transition rules.

## Standing

The 21 of Coherence already provides the triadic source structure:

- 7 Constraints
- 7 Agreements
- 7 Resolutions of Coherence

The Seed Concordance preserves native distinction and seats the Resolutions as the lived coherence outcomes that become visible when coherence is maintained. The current conversion-side isomorph therefore stands as:

- Gates ↔ Constraints

- MEs ↔ Resolutions

This standing is correspondence, not count equivalence across unlike layers.

## Relation Statement

MEs correspond to Resolutions because MEs function as the conversion-side measure family through which coherence becomes embodied, inscribed, and governable in structured form, while the Resolutions define what coherence becomes when it is actually maintained.

In conversion-side relation, MEs do not merely symbolize Resolutions.
They stand in family correspondence to them as the measurable embodiment layer through which lived coherence outcomes become stable, legible, and structurally seated.

## What This Means

MEs are the conversion-side measure family.
Resolutions are the source-side lived outcome family.

Their relation is isomorphic at the family level:

- Resolutions describe what coherence becomes in lived truth
- MEs hold structured embodiment of that becoming
- Resolutions show the non-collapsed outcomes of maintained coherence
- MEs provide the conversion-side measure surface through which those outcomes can stand in ordered form

This permits coherent relation without forcing premature one-to-one assignment between individual MEs and individual Resolutions.

## Boundaries
