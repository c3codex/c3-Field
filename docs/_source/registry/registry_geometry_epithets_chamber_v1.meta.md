# Registry Geometry — Chamber of Epithets

## Purpose

Seat the ordering geometry of the Chamber of Epithets.

## Structural Geometry

The Chamber resolves as a 9-seat sequence released in 3 / 3 / 3.

This geometry preserves:
- ordered progression
- explicit seat identity
- public release distinction
- chamber integrity across materials and role-bearing Epithets

## Release Geometry

- Spring Equinox
- seats 1 through 3

### Second triad

### Third triad

## Native Distinction

This geometry does not collapse:
- Codex existence
- Field structure
- Measures registration
- public announcement

A seat may exist in Codex and be structured in Field before its public reveal state resolves.

Lapis is valid in the Chamber where required by seated role and material truth.
The Chamber is not limited to oracle-card-only material logic.
