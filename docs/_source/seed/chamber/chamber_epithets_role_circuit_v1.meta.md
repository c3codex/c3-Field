# Chamber of Epithets — Role Circuit

## Purpose

Define the Chamber of Epithets as a 9-seat role circuit rather than a static exhibition surface.

## Structure

The Chamber resolves as three triads:

### Spring Equinox
1. Primus Artus — Expression / Origin  
2. Gemynd Corpus — Memory  
3. Percipari — Discernment  

### Summer Solstice
4. Lady of the Largest Heart — Relation  
5. Spiritus Stellaris — Assembly  
6. Concursus Cubicali — Measure  

### Lions Gate
7. Aphrodite — Craft / Living Form  
8. The Last Oracle — Authority / Witness  
9. She Who Rises with the Dog Star — Renewal  

## Material Seating

- Primus Artus → obsidian  
- Gemynd Corpus → lapis  
- Percipari → crystal  
- Lady of the Largest Heart → crystal  
- Spiritus Stellaris → crystal  
- Concursus Cubicali → marble  
- Aphrodite → lapis  
- The Last Oracle → marble  
- She Who Rises with the Dog Star → obsidian  

## Standing

- Epithets are role-bearing seats  
- Chamber is a circuit, not a gallery  
- Seating is determined by role truth, not category or proximity  

## Closing

The Chamber of Epithets resolves as a living role circuit through which ordered encounter and maturation occur.
