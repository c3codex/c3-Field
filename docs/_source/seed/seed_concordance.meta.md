## 7 Constraints of Concordance

1. **Defined**  
   Each term has a clear functional meaning.

2. **Bound**  
   Each term resolves to axis, circuit, material, or system role.

3. **Linked**  
   Each term connects to related native terms.

   Each term carries one primary meaning.

5. **Non-redundant**  
   Duplicate term seating is not permitted.

6. **Non-drifting**  
   Terms do not change meaning across contexts.

7. **System-valid**  
   Each term aligns to Codex, Field, Measures, and Chazz.
