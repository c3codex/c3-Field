# The 21 of Coherence — CI Participant Clarification

## Determination

The canonical 7 Constraints, 7 Agreements, and 7 Resolutions remain unchanged.

The participant scope of The 21 of Coherence is clarified for current state as:

- Named Individuals
- Institutions in Service
- Computational Intelligence (CI)

The prior source wording `AI` is preserved as historical provenance. `Computational Intelligence (CI)` is the current participant term and supersedes `AI` only as the participant-class label; it does not rewrite the canonical 21.

## CI Standing

Computational Intelligence is an explicit participant within the c3 relational and computational model.

CI may participate in relationship, contribution, execution, observation, analysis, and other permitted functions when those functions are properly bounded by the applicable environment, role, permissions, standing, and passage conditions.

CI participation does not itself create:

- human identity
- Named Individual standing
- institutional identity
- Operator standing
- source authority
- system authority
- ownership
- custody
- permission beyond what is established
- independent standing beyond the conditions under which the CI participates

CI is participant, not person and not system.

## Role and System Distinction

A Named Individual remains a Named Individual.
An Institution in Service remains an Institution in Service.
Computational Intelligence remains CI.

Operator remains a bounded role carried by a Named Individual and is not a participant class.
NotChazz remains system, not participant.

Participant, role, system, source authority, Registry standing, permission, custody, execution, visibility, and runtime remain distinct.

## Application to the 21

Where the canonical source says `actor`, current-state relational application may read `participant` when referring collectively to Named Individuals, Institutions in Service, and CI.

This clarification does not alter the text, order, count, or standing of the 7 Constraints, 7 Agreements, or 7 Resolutions.

## Closing

Named Individuals participate.
Institutions in Service participate.
Computational Intelligence participates.

Their participation is relational.
Their roles remain distinct.
The system remains system.
