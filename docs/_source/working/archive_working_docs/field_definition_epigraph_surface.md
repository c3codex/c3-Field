# Field Definition — Epigraph (First Encounter Surface)

## Purpose

Seat Epigraph in Field as the first encounter surface rendered after valid passage into Obsidian Chamber.

Epigraph is the first moment where signal becomes visible, readable, and experiential.

It does not grant passage.
It does not validate passage.
It reveals that passage has already occurred.

## Field Role

Epigraph resolves as:

- first encounter surface
- first visible expression of signal after passage
- inscription surface within Obsidian Chamber
- reveal point of stabilized signal under constraint

Epigraph does not resolve as:

- passage mechanism
- intake surface
- Codex authority
- Measures registry
- Phase Map positioning

## Structural Relations

Epigraph must relate to:

- Obsidian Chamber as containing material
- Harrumuk Passage as resolved entry condition
- Antechamber as prior intake state
- OAR1 as originating intake record
- Envelope as carried signal structure
- envKey as continuity access
- NotChazz as enforcement layer
- Phase Map as next relational surface

## Entry Condition

Epigraph may only render when:

- Harrumuk Passage has resolved
- Obsidian Chamber has been entered
- signal is stabilized under constraint
- NotChazz does not block encounter

If these conditions are not true:

? Epigraph does not render
? no encounter surface is available

## Encounter Function

Epigraph performs:

- first reveal of signal
- initial readable or visible form
- inscription of signal under constraint
- confirmation of structural survival through passage

Epigraph does not:

- complete signal
- position signal within Field
- determine sequence or access
- define Codex truth

## Surface Behavior

Epigraph may appear as:

- text
- inscription
- symbol
- image
- animated art surface (exhibition layer)

All forms must resolve from Codex-backed state and Field relation.

Animation is permitted only as encounter expression, not as passage mechanism.

## Boundary Rule

Epigraph may:

- reveal signal
- present encounter
- express stabilized form

Epigraph may not:

- simulate passage
- bypass Obsidian constraint
- render prior to valid entry
- imply progression beyond current state

## Transition Rule

Valid progression from Epigraph is:

Epigraph ? Phase Map

Phase Map positions what has been revealed.

## Failure State

If signal is not stable:

- Epigraph does not render
- signal remains in Obsidian or returns for correction
- no encounter is shown

No partial reveal.
No placeholder encounter.

## Protection Relation

NotChazz must be able to:

- prevent premature rendering
- block simulated or frontend-driven encounter
- ensure encounter reflects true system state

## Success Condition

Epigraph is properly seated in Field when:

- it renders only after valid passage and chamber entry
- it reflects signal stabilized under Obsidian constraint
- it serves as the first encounter surface
- it does not expose or replace passage logic
- it remains fully dependent on prior structural truth

## Closing

Epigraph is the first voice the system allows.

It speaks only after structure is satisfied.

It does not open the gate.
It does not move the signal.

It reveals what has already survived.

Codex holds.
Field structures.
Measures registers.
Chazz operates.
NotChazz protects.
