# TREE Concordance Extension Proposal

## Purpose

Seat TREE terminology as proposed native semantics without mutating readonly Seed Concordance directly.

TREE enters as a Field / Encounter relational environment term.

---

## TREE

Traced Rooted Encounter Environment

TREE is the coherent living environment through which rooted systems become encounterable without losing trace, relation, or authority continuity.

TREE does not replace:
- Codex
- Field
- Measures
- OAR
- Chazz
- rooted execution spine

TREE conditions encounter.
The trunk routes execution.

- Axis: Field
- Circuit: c1 c2 c3
- Role: traced rooted encounter environment
- Resolves to: coherent living relation across rooted branches

---

## Environment

The coherent living relation within which encounter occurs.

Environment allows Field relation to become visible without exposing rooted authority continuity directly.

- Axis: Field
- Role: coherent living relation
- Resolves to: visible relational coherence

---

## Canopy

The traced communication layer across the environment.

Canopy preserves communication continuity, traceability, and encounter relation.

Canopy does not function as centralized command authority.

- Axis: encounter
- Role: traced communication layer
- Resolves to: traceable communication continuity

---

## Boundary

The relational access governance layer.

Boundary governs:
- who may access
- what may be accessed
- encounter depth
- standing relation
- branch eligibility

Boundary does not replace rooted execution authority.

- Axis: governance
- Role: relational access governance
- Resolves to: bounded relational access

---

## Branch

A unique operational encounter expression rooted in shared continuity.

Branches may differ in:
- encounter style
- purpose
- operational behavior
- aesthetics
- participant relation

Branches may not establish separate roots.

- Axis: encounter
- Role: operational encounter expression
- Resolves to: rooted branch operation

---

## Trunk

The rooted execution spine.

Current trunk:

    Codex -> Field -> Measures -> OAR2 -> Chazz -> Cody -> src

The trunk routes execution continuity between roots and branches.

- Axis: execution
- Role: rooted execution spine
- Resolves to: routed operational continuity

---

## Roots

Codex continuity, provenance, authority lineage, and seeded memory.

Roots hold continuity beneath visible encounter surfaces.

- Axis: authority
- Role: rooted continuity holder
- Resolves to: persistent authority continuity

---

## Non-Collapse Rule

TREE may not collapse into:
- dashboard
- orchestration layer
- workflow engine
- alternate authority structure
- process replacement
- execution console

TREE conditions encounter.

The trunk routes execution.

---

## Closing

Codex holds.
Field structures.
Measures registers.
Chazz executes.

TREE makes the Field visible without exposing the Roots.
