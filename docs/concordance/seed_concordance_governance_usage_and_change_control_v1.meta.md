# Seed Concordance Governance, Usage, and Change Control v1

## Purpose

The Seed Concordance is the governed semantic reference surface for the c3 Field.

Its purpose is to:

- preserve native semantic distinction
- stabilize operational meaning
- prevent semantic drift
- maintain role and authority boundaries
- support Codex-seated execution integrity
- provide shared semantic continuity across Codex, Field, Measures, Chazz, Cody, and `src`

The Seed Concordance is not:

- a glossary
- branding language
- philosophical freeform
- frontend copywriting
- runtime invention surface
- mutable collaborative terminology

The Seed Concordance exists to preserve coherent operational semantics.

---

# Authority Order

Native authority order remains:

    Codex → Field → Measures → Chazz

Concordance terms may describe authority.
They do not replace authority.

The Concordance is:

- Codex-seated semantic infrastructure
- append-governed semantic reference
- relation-aware semantic continuity

---

# Usage Rules

## 1. Native distinction must remain intact

The Concordance preserves distinction between:

    authority
    structure
    registry
    execution
    actor
    role
    routing
    visibility
    state
    verification
    recognition

Terms may relate.
They may not collapse.

---

## 2. One primary meaning per term

Each seated term holds:

- one primary operational meaning
- one primary semantic role
- one bounded semantic standing

Alias sprawl and duplicate semantic seating are prohibited.

---

## 3. Frontend is not authority

Frontend, UI, snapshots, markdown, and runtime state do not redefine Concordance standing.

    snapshot != authority
    markdown != authority
    frontend != authority

Only Codex-seated standing governs operational meaning.

---

## 4. Concordance terms are operational

Terms are seated because they affect:

- routing
- reveal
- verification
- relation
- governance
- execution
- conversion
- protection
- encounter behavior

Conceptual or decorative language does not enter Concordance authority.

---

## 5. Protected systems intelligence remains protected

Protected Systems Intelligence may be:

- declared
- structured
- referenced
- bounded
- invoked

It may not be exposed as reconstructible public execution body.

# Change Control

## 1. No direct mutation

Seated Concordance authority is append-governed.

Existing semantic authority may not be silently rewritten.

Changes require:

    review
    validation
    OAR2 routing
    execution
    validation
    OAR1 closeout

---

## 2. New terms require recurrence

A term should not be seated because it sounds useful.

A term must demonstrate:

- operational recurrence
- bounded meaning
- non-duplication
- structural necessity
- stable relation
- native alignment

before seating.

---

## 3. Audit before expansion

When semantic density increases, audit occurs before continued expansion.

Audit categories include:

- duplicate active labels
- orphan relations
- unresolved references
- inactive versions
- protected exposure
- semantic collisions
- authority drift
- missing standing

---

## 4. Process architecture is not automatically semantic authority

Operational or architectural language does not automatically become Concordance authority.

Possible standings include:

    seat
    defer
    classify_non_term
    classify_protected

TREE-style architecture language must remain explicitly dispositioned.

---

## 5. Scope neutrality preserved

Relations remain:

    scope-neutral
    append-safe
    metadata-compatible
    non-hardcoded

Semantic seating must not over-specialize the substrate prematurely.

---

# Recovery and Correction

If semantic drift or invalid seating occurs:

- correct locally
- preserve append continuity
- avoid broad rollback
- avoid schema panic
- avoid silent repair

Correction routes through:

    verification
    bounded correction OAR2
    validation
    OAR1 closeout

Localized correction is preferred over global mutation.

---

# Success Condition

The Concordance is functioning correctly when:

- semantic distinctions remain stable
- authority boundaries remain clear
- duplicate meanings do not proliferate
- operational relations remain coherent
- frontend does not invent truth
- execution remains role-bound
- verification precedes recognition
- protected systems intelligence remains protected
- Codex-seated meaning governs runtime behavior

---

Nothing enters Concordance because it is interesting.

Terms enter Concordance because coherence, routing, governance, execution, or relation depend upon them.

Codex holds.
Field structures.
Measures registers.
Chazz executes.
