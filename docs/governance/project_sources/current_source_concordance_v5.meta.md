# Current Source Concordance — v5

## Purpose

Maintain complete, authoritative semantic language across the c3 Field, Measures Registry, Project-source governance, governed execution, custody, runtime, and review surfaces.

## Concordance Conditions

Every operative term must be defined, bound, linked, singular, non-redundant, non-drifting, and system-valid.

## Native Relation

`Codex → Field → Measures → OAR2 → Registrar → OAR1 → Chazz → Operator`

* Codex records authority.
* Field structures relation and boundary.
* Measures registers identity, sequence, access, evidence, and standing.
* OAR2 carries execution-specific authority.
* Registrar acts within the confirmed instruction.
* OAR1 returns objective result and evidence.
* Chazz reviews, summarizes, and recommends standing.
* Operator validates and disposes at assigned touch points.

No surface silently inherits another surface's authority.

## Foundational Terms

### Coherence

Source condition recognized through maintained right relation, distinction, valid passage, ordered progression, living memory, integrity, and non-collapse. The system aligns to and maintains Coherence; it does not manufacture it.

### c3 Model

`Connect → Contribute → Create`

The c3 Model identifies the three minimal functions of a coherent environment. Lowercase `c1`, `c2`, and `c3` identify their functional relational circuits in the shared Field.

The c3 Model is not an authorization sequence and must not be collapsed into the Commerce Currents, OAR lifecycle, or SRC onboarding relation.

### Modeled Initiative

Initiative formed in relation to Connect, Contribute, and Create, with authority, evidence, passage, and standing preserved through the applicable governed records. An initiative is not Modeled merely because work began or an implementation exists.

### c3 7s

Canonical short name for the complete 21 of Coherence: seven Constraints, seven Agreements, and seven Resolutions. The two names identify one inseparable source set.

### Codex

Recorded authority surface. Recording authority is not execution.

### Field

Relational structure within which roles, systems, assets, passages, and boundaries are recognized.

### Measures

Registry function recording identity, sequence, access, evidence, and standing. Registration does not necessarily require full document storage and does not equal activation.

### Registry

Governed record of identity, relation, version, standing, evidence, and applicable custody or activation bindings. Registry is not merely storage, and registration does not itself execute, activate, verify, certify, or publish.

### Computational Systems Governance

Governance of the computational system and operational environment inherited by AI and other computational actors. It governs authority, roles, processes, data, interfaces, custody, execution, evidence, review, and change conditions within the system rather than treating the AI model alone as the governed object.

### Operator

Bounded Named Individual role holding assigned validation and disposition touch points. For this Project, the Operator is `op044` unless explicitly changed.

### Chazz

Systems intelligence and advisory/review role. Chazz forms, identifies drift, reviews evidence, summarizes, and recommends standing. Execution requires explicit assignment and cannot silently impersonate independent review.

### Registrar

Named AI or system role executing confirmed OAR2 authority within the approved execution boundary. Registrar replaces generic Executor terminology where the operative function is governed registration, normalization, transfer, placement, mutation, or related execution.

## Compact and Contract Standing

### Compact

The governed relational standing carried through Constraint, Agreement, and Resolution. A compact establishes the boundary, conditions, and enduring outcomes of participation.

* Constraint defines the boundary.
* Agreement permits participation.
* Resolution carries what endures.

A compact is no longer in standing when its constraint is violated, its agreement is broken, or its resolution can no longer be carried.

### Contract

Non-preferred term for governed architectural relations due to multi-domain ambiguity (legal, software interface, schema, business, system promises). Preferred native terms include constraint, agreement, resolution, compact, standing, boundary, requirement, and passage. Legacy implementation terminology containing "contract" does not supersede native architectural language.

## Structured Registry Contribution

### SRC

Structured Registry Contribution. A structured asset-contribution and onboarding relation under development. SRC1, SRC2, and SRC3 identify distinct contribution classes.

### SRC1

Structured contact and communications contribution within the Obsidian Chamber relation, encompassing contact capture, subscriptions, email updates, and communication preferences. It does not independently grant system access, operational authority, or progression standing.

### SRC2

Structured material contribution identifying and relating material expressions such as artwork, publications, documents, media, research, and governed assets. Requires a valid contributing identity via `c3_key` or `op_key`. Staged assets remain in staging custody until a confirmed OAR2 authorizes a Registrar to transfer them to a destination.

### SRC3

Structured DAO contribution. Standing remains held pending development of the DAO contribution architecture.

## Identity and Operational Permission

### c3_key

Identity-bound participant key carrying registered attributes such as permitted environments, contribution permissions, progression standing, custody, and submission eligibility. A `c3_key` does not possess inherent authority.

### op_key

Operator-qualified identity key used for bounded operational participation inside c3Ops. It carries attributes permitting actions such as entering environments, opening operational encounters, forming/resuming SRC2 work, requesting Chazz assistance, validating OAR2, and receiving review.

### role_key

Identifies a system-held, service-held, or AI-held operational role (e.g., Chazz assistance, Registrar execution, system services, role-bound branch AI). Human permissions are carried as registered attributes of `c3_key` or `op_key` rather than separate role keys.

## OP Encounter for SRC2

### OP SRC2 Encounter

A c3Ops operational encounter within the Measures Registry environment and Lapis Antechamber allowing an authorized `op_key` to form/resume an SRC2, identify target registered systems, stage associated material assets, prepare an asset manifest, specify custody, and compose an OAR2 proposal.

### Asset Manifest

The governed record identifying the assets associated with an SRC2, specifying asset identity, version, media class, source location, source custody, content hash, required destination, transfer mode, and applicable mutation boundary.

### Staging Custody

The temporary governed custody in which assets remain while their identity, evidence, destination, and authorized treatment are being formed.

### Destination Custody

The governed location and standing established after an authorized Registrar action places or transfers an asset to its required destination. Requires objective evidence.

## OAR Records

### OAR2

Observed, Aligned, Routed. The Operator–AI-derived outbound authority record for one bounded execution, identifying observed conditions, aligned authority, exact route, Registrar, execution instance, destination, mutation boundary, and return requirements.

### OAR1

Objective, Action, Result. For governed repository artifacts, OAR1 is the artifact-bound append-only AI execution history documenting actual execution, result, evidence, mutations, custody, omissions, held conditions, and commit/hash lineage.

Each material AI creation or mutation appends one immutable execution entry. The OAR1 history may grow; prior execution entries may not be silently rewritten, removed, replaced, or collapsed. A correction appends a new entry identifying what it corrects.

OAR1 proves artifact-level execution. It does not originate authority, accept the result, close the cycle, or authorize the next action.

### OAR3

Bounded Operator–AI record for deployed AI participation inside a non-native branch system. It allows Optics to record the externally relevant Operator–AI request and participation standing without intruding into the branch system's protected internal implementation.

### AI Execution Capability Profile

Environment-qualified c3Ops record of demonstrated technical operations available to a named AI execution relation. Capability is not authority and may not be inferred from model identity.

Initial capabilities are `read`, `draft`, `edit_local`, `edit_repo`, `commit`, `push`, `registry_read`, `registry_write`, `external_call`, `return_evidence`, and `oar1_append`.

**Authority never exceeds demonstrated capability, and capability never implies authority.**

### Artifact-Bound OAR1 Sidecar

Adjacent repository record named by default `<artifact-filename>.oar1.meta.md`, carrying append-only AI execution lineage for one governed artifact. Artifact and sidecar travel together in Git custody. The sidecar does not independently create Registry standing or execution authority.

## Canopy Communications

### Canopy

Governed relation to non-native application surfaces.

### Canopy Communications (CanCom)

The governed communications and transfer interface preserving identity, routing, custody, integrity, collision refusal, and return evidence. Used when separate AI roles, operational surfaces, services, or non-native functions cannot communicate through one shared native environment.

### CanCom Passage Boundary

The boundary crossed when a governed communication leaves one operational surface and becomes available to another authorized surface or role. CanCom passage changes the operational surface and may change the applicable AI or system role. Every CanCom passage event automatically creates an Optics observational record.

## Optics

### Optics

The shared system function that records operational data at governed boundaries. It automatically records the regenerated semantic authority, updated Source Authority Index, and historical concordance preservation. It does not require Operator initiation for observational recording.

### Optics Review

The system-level comparison of authorized intention, observed passage, returned evidence, timing, custody, and standing.

## NotChazz

### NotChazz

System intelligence and constraint relation operating over registered and observed system data. NotChazz evaluates expected operational state against observed operational evidence. If any required semantic section is omitted, if the authority hierarchy is inconsistent, or if historical evidence replaces the regenerated authority source, NotChazz generates a SEND record.

## Protected OAR Custody and Retention

### Branch-Held OAR Content

Full OAR packages remain under branch custody to protect sensitive instructions, proprietary workflows, and branch-specific details.

### Optics Observational Record

Optics retains a compact observational record containing minimum evidence fields (such as record key, type, branch key, content hash, standing, destination, notchazz flags, and event timestamps) bound to the branch-held content via cryptographic hashes.

### Archive Custody

The archival relation preserving original package hashes, compressed archive hashes, package manifests, and retrieval rules under governed retention rules.

## SEND and send.card

### SEND

The shared governed communications, grievance, exception, supplemental evidence, and resolution-routing service.

### send.card

The immutable governed communications artifact carrying Constraint, Agreement, Resolution, and Disposition (CARD).

* Constraint identifies the applicable boundary, requirement, conflict, or grievance.
* Agreement identifies the recognized participation, expectation, or proposed relation.
* Resolution identifies what has been determined, evidenced, answered, corrected, or carried forward.
* Disposition identifies the required next standing or route.

## Historical and Held Terms

### Isomorphism

Operative functional relational reasoning that recognizes a valid structural relation across different contexts or states without declaring those contexts identical. Isomorphism permits translation of relation while preserving each context's identity, constraints, and standing.

### Isomorphic Appearance

Visible or descriptive similarity without sufficient evidence of functional relational equivalence. Isomorphic Appearance must not be treated as proof of Isomorphism.

## Collision Rule

Qualify terms when domain meanings could collide. Lowercase c1/c2/c3 relational circuits, uppercase C1/C2/C3 Commerce Currents, OAR1/OAR2 relations, and SRC1/SRC2/SRC3 records must remain distinct.

## Unknown Standing Rule

When a registry record, hash, custody claim, runtime activation, or implementation cannot be verified, declare it unknown or held. Do not reconstruct it from historical prose.

## Boundary

This Concordance defines language after approval. It does not itself register records, activate runtimes, authorize execution, mutate SEAT, or dispose of evidence.
