# Pre-existing Structural Drift Frame — Field Findings 2026-W28

## Purpose
Preserve the July 2026 structural-drift analytical frame as pre-existing evidence before current healthcare GTM case analysis, so later cases can test the frame without silently rewriting it to fit observed results.

This file does not modify the July publication, grant it new authority, or establish GTM/Registry standing. It records the frame as reference-only analytical provenance for falsification.

## Provenance
Source observation window: July 4–10, 2026.

Source public derivative: `field_findings_2026_w28_public_article_v2`.

The source's central finding was:

> Organizations are assigning autonomous capability faster than they are establishing the operational environments required to govern it.

The source further concluded that autonomous AI cannot be governed solely at the model layer because consequential behavior occurs through relationships with operational environments.

## Seven Structural Drift Indicators — Preserved Frame

1. **Ownership Drift** — Agents operate without a clearly accountable owner or with ownership fragmented among IT, security, engineering, vendors, and business units.
2. **Authority Drift** — An agent's technical capability or inherited access is mistaken for legitimate authority.
3. **Position Drift** — An AI is assigned an objective or operational function without evaluating whether that function should exist, how it contributes to the institution, or what harm may result if it is executed exactly as assigned.
4. **Identity Drift** — Human accounts, service identities, shared credentials, workload identities, and agent identities are used inconsistently.
5. **Runtime Drift** — Governance exists in policies and meetings but is not represented in the environment where the agent acts.
6. **Evidence Drift** — Organizations cannot reconstruct what the agent did, what information influenced it, why it acted, or which authority permitted the action.
7. **Response Drift** — Human review and incident response remain slower than the computational systems they are expected to govern.

## Original Environmental Review Relation
The July source associated the frame with environmental review of:
- existing AI and agent inventory;
- ownership;
- authority boundaries;
- identity;
- access;
- dependency mapping;
- runtime visibility;
- interruption controls;
- evidence and traceability;
- human approval points.

## Falsification Rule
For each external case, map evidence to the seven indicators using only:
- `direct_fit`
- `partial_fit`
- `no_fit`
- `insufficient_evidence`

Do not create a fit by broadening an indicator after the fact.

A case condition that does not fit must be preserved as a mismatch candidate. Repeated mismatches may indicate that the frame is incomplete or that a new distinction is required.
