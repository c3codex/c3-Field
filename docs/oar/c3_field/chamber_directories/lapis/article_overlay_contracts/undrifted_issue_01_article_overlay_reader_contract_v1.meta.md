# unDrifted Issue 01 Article Overlay Reader Contract v1

## Overlay Behavior

    dispatches open as overlay reader above /undrifted
    /undrifted remains parent surface
    cover remains visible behind or underneath overlay
    no full page navigation unless later canonical article route is registered

## Overlay Reader Must Include

    article title
    article media
    article body or excerpt
    close/back to issue
    accessible focus state
    escape-to-close behavior

## Articles Included

    Structural Drift
    Agents with Keys
    Agents of Chaos

## Disallowed Overlay Behavior

    route away from /undrifted by default
    replace page state without back/close control
    open raw Paragraph URL as first behavior
    expose internal material/chamber language
    expose C1/C2/C3
    expose commerce/pricing/payment

## Future Extension Allowed After Later OAR

    canonical article route
    Paragraph canonical source link
    share/copy link
    Buffer/social preview link

Only after later OAR.
