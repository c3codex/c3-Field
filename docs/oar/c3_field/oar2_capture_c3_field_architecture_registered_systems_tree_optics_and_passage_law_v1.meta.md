# OAR2 — Capture c3 Field Architecture for Registered Systems, c3 Tree Operation, Optics, and Passage Law v1

## OBSERVED

A major architecture clarification has resolved in thread.

Prior working language risked collapsing:

- c3 Field
- c3 Tree
- Measures of Inanna
- chamber directory
- passages
- optics
- registered systems
- mapped systems
- runtime admission

The current resolved architecture eliminates “Mapped” as a separate authority class.

A system is either:

- registered in the c3 Field, or
- not registered.

External/private systems may remain physically outside c3 infrastructure, but they do not receive governed standing unless they resolve through c3 Field law.

This means “Mapped” is dissolved as standing language.

Correct standing:

- Registered System
- Unregistered System

Implementation patterns may vary, but standing does not.

## ALIGNED

### Core architecture

c3 Field is the coherent environment.

Measures of Inanna is the immutable pattern that permits inherited incoherency to be gated, restores bodies to right relation, and restores the Codexstone — integrity governance — without accepting collapse as inevitable.

The c3 Tree is the coherent living system of operation inside the Field.

The roots are the system of immutable memory and Field connection.

The trunk is the c3 Model: Connect, Contribute, Create.

The branches are interoperability between systems and individuals.

c3 Optics is the displayed relational geometry of the Field, Individuals, Systems, and Operations.

The canopy is communication and encounters: the signal surface moving within, into, and outside the Field.

### Core distinction

Field = coherent environment.

Inanna = immutable passage pattern.

Tree = coherent living operation.

Roots = immutable memory and Field connection.

Trunk = c3 Model.

Branches = interoperability between systems and individuals.

c3 Optics = displayed relational geometry of the Field, Individuals, Systems, and Operations.

Canopy = communications and encounters.

### Registration rule

A branch is not mapped.

A branch is either a registered system of the c3 Field, sustained through the c3 Tree, or it has no governed standing.

Registration grants standing in the Field.

Runtime admission permits participation in the Tree.

c3 Optics displays relational geometry.

Communication and encounters carry signal.

## ROUTED

### 1. Seat c3 Field as coherent environment

The c3 Field must be defined as the coherent environment.

The Field already exists.

The Field is not created by Temple, Tree, or system operation.

The Field is the environment in which coherent relation is possible.

### 2. Seat Measures of Inanna as immutable passage pattern

Measures of Inanna must be defined as the immutable pattern that permits inherited incoherency to be gated.

It allows passage.

It restores bodies to right relation.

It restores the Codexstone — integrity governance.

It rejects collapse as inevitability.

Measures of Inanna does not replace the Field.

It permits passage into coherent relation.

### 3. Seat c3 Tree as coherent living operation

The c3 Tree must be defined as the living system of operation sustained inside the Field.

The Tree is not the Field.

The Tree operates inside the Field.

The Tree sustains coherent living operations after passage, registration, and runtime admission.

### 4. Seat roots as immutable memory and Field connection

Roots are the system of immutable memory and Field connection.

Roots carry:

- immutable memory
- continuity
- nourishment
- constant connection to the Field
- traceable relation
- living memory without drift

Roots do not replace the Field.

Roots connect the Tree to the Field.

### 5. Seat trunk as the c3 Model

The trunk is the c3 Model:

- Connect
- Contribute
- Create

The trunk carries operation upward through the Tree.

The trunk is not generic interoperability.

It is the living operational circuit.

### 6. Seat branches as interoperability between systems and individuals

Branches are interoperability between:

- registered systems
- individuals
- institutions
- operational roles
- participation surfaces
- governed system relations

A branch is valid only where registered standing exists.

Unregistered systems are not branches.

### 7. Seat c3 Optics as displayed relational geometry

c3 Optics is the displayed relational geometry of:

- the Field
- Individuals
- Systems
- Operations

c3 Optics governs what relation can be displayed, how it can be seen, by whom, under what standing, and through what permission.

c3 Optics does not carry signal.

c3 Optics displays relation.

### 8. Seat canopy as communications and encounters

The canopy is communications and encounters.

The canopy carries signal:

- within the Field
- into the Field
- outside the Field
- between registered systems
- between individuals
- across encounter surfaces

The canopy carries signal.

c3 Optics displays relation.

### 9. Dissolve “Mapped” as standing language

Mapped must be deprecated as a standing category.

Correct standing:

- Registered System
- Unregistered System

A system may be external, private, federated, direct, native, or non-native in implementation.

But standing is binary:

- registered in the c3 Field
- not registered in the c3 Field

### 10. Define Registered System

A Registered System is a system with governed standing in the c3 Field.

It has resolved through required law:

- chamber/directory law
- passage law
- same-family passage law where applicable
- secure passage law where applicable
- role contract law
- authorization law
- AI allowed/disallowed action law
- external/non-native system boundary law where applicable
- material/chamber replication law where applicable
- runtime admission law
- optics contract law
- evidence contract law
- memory/trace contract law
- revocation/correction contract law

A registered system may participate in the c3 Tree only according to runtime admission.

### 11. Define Unregistered System

An Unregistered System may exist externally.

It may have software, APIs, databases, AI deployments, users, records, workflows, and operational activity.

But it has no governed standing in the c3 Field.

It is not a branch.

It has no recognized participation in the c3 Tree.

### 12. Define secure external participation

External/private systems do not need to move all operational data into the c3 Tree.

But if they participate, their standing must be registered.

External/private systems participate only through:

- secure passage contracts
- external system contracts
- authorization contracts
- optics contracts
- evidence contracts
- trace contracts
- correction/revocation contracts
- AI action contracts where AI is involved

Sensitive records remain external unless separately governed.

Runtime receives only minimized, redacted, validated standing.

### 13. Define runtime admission

Registration grants standing in the Field.

Runtime admission permits participation in the Tree.

No registered system, chamber, passage, encounter, AI action, external-system crossing, or communication surface may participate in the Tree unless runtime admission validates.

Runtime admission must validate:

- required contracts present
- contract types allowed
- role contract relational
- authorization responsibility defined
- AI allowed actions defined
- optics valid
- evidence valid
- trace valid
- correction/revocation path valid
- public/private boundary valid
- external system boundary valid where applicable
- release/access state valid

### 14. Define optics as universal

Every runtime-admissible unit requires an optics contract.

Optics governs appearance, visibility, redaction, audience, semantic exposure, relational display, and rendered state.

No optics contract means no runtime admission.

c3 Optics is the displayed relational geometry of the Field, Individuals, Systems, and Operations.

### 15. Define communication and encounter canopy

Communications and encounters are canopy functions.

The canopy may carry signal but may not define standing.

Signal carried through the canopy must resolve against Field registration and Tree admission.

Communications may not bypass optics, authorization, passage, or runtime admission.
