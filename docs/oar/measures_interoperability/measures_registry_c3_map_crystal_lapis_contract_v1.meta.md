# Measures Registry — Crystal/Lapis c3 MAP Contract v1

## Contract Purpose

The Crystal/Lapis c3 MAP Chamber is the recognition and relation surface of the Left Path.

c3 MAP = Measures Assessment Protocol.

This chamber explains the Measures Registry, surfaces pattern visibility, and qualifies the visitor's asset/state pattern for delivery contract routing.

## c3 MAP Definition

**c3 MAP = Measures Assessment Protocol**

c3 MAP does not price.

c3 MAP does not assign commerce.

c3 MAP assesses and qualifies.

Qualified assets may enter Commerced Circuits (C1 / C2 / C3).

The qualification happens through the Obsidian Assessment Gate. c3 MAP is the chamber that explains and introduces the qualification sequence.

## Material Assignment

Crystal/Lapis dual assignment.

Crystal = recognition, clarity, pattern visibility.

Lapis = relational positioning, structural orientation.

The c3 MAP Chamber is where recognition and relation operate together.

The visitor sees their pattern. The system receives their standing.

## Chamber Functions

### 1. Recognition Surface

The c3 MAP Chamber surfaces the visitor's AI operational pattern.

It does not generate the pattern. It makes the visitor aware of what their environment reveals.

Crystal function: clarity. The visitor sees what is already there.

### 2. Relation Establishment

The c3 MAP Chamber confirms the relational contract between:
- The visitor's institutional context
- The Measures Registry system
- The SRC surface (structured response contract)

Lapis function: the visitor is placed within the system.

### 3. c3 MAP Explanation

The chamber explains:
- What Measures Registry is (a registered environment for governing AI behavior)
- What c3 MAP does (assesses and qualifies the asset/state pattern)
- What comes next (the Obsidian Assessment Gate)
- What qualification unlocks (Commerced Circuit entry)

### 4. Pattern Visibility

The chamber surfaces the visitor's observable pattern before they enter the Assessment Gate.

It does not assign a standing. It does not diagnose.

It makes the pattern visible so the visitor can enter the gate with awareness.

### 5. SRC Intake

Before the visitor enters the Obsidian Assessment Gate, SRC intake is required.

SRC intake fields (from `connect_src` surface):
- `institution_name`
- `institution_type`
- `contact_name`
- `contact_email`

SRC intake is the threshold between relational positioning and assessment entry.

The `connectSrcNextEncounter` state determines whether the visitor proceeds to `measures_assessment` or `structured_eval`.

### 6. Delivery Contract Qualification

After the visitor completes the Assessment Gate, the c3 MAP qualification is resolved.

Qualified states:
- `structured_governance_candidate` — high qualification, Marble Circuit eligible
- Other standing states as defined in `assessment_interpretation` standing rules

Qualified assets enter C1 / C2 / C3 Commerced Circuits. Those circuits require delivery contracts before activation.

## What c3 MAP Must Not Do

- Price the asset or visitor
- Assign final commerce by itself
- Replace the Obsidian Assessment Gate
- Claim assessment completion before the gate is passed
- Expose condition tags directly to the visitor

## Continuation

From the c3 MAP Chamber, the visitor proceeds to the Obsidian Assessment Gate.

SRC intake (`connect_src`) is the threshold crossing.

## Boundary

This contract governs c3 MAP definition, recognition function, and SRC intake threshold.

C1 / C2 / C3 Commerced Circuit activation is governed by the Marble Commerced Circuit Contract.

Runtime implementation is a separate OAR2.

No media, CSS, or DB mutation is authorized by this contract.

## Close

Crystal recognizes.

Lapis relates.

c3 MAP qualifies.

The Obsidian Gate assesses.
