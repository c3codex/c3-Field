# Measures Registry — Chamber Tone Contract v1

## Contract Purpose

The Chamber Tone Contract defines the tone of each material place in the Measures Registry installation: its qualities, function, voice, and what it must not do.

Tone carries chamber state. Tone does not author truth.

No chamber tone may imply authority, recognition, payment, conversion, or Commerced Circuit activation.

Tone supports encounter state only.

## Global Chamber Tone Rule

| Material | Tone Function |
|---|---|
| Obsidian | exposes |
| Crystal | clarifies |
| Lapis | relates |
| Marble | governs |
| Media | carries |
| Temple | branches |
| Epigraph | opens |

### Global Tone Register

Measures Registry tone is:

- institutional
- calm
- precise
- trust-bearing
- audit-grade
- museum-grade
- quietly geometric
- non-hype
- non-fantasy
- non-fear-selling

**Lock phrase: Quiet authority. Visible structure. Governed passage.**

This tone register applies across all material places. Individual place tone extends from this register — it does not override it.

## Place Tone Definitions

### 1. Epigraph Place

**Tone:**
- restrained
- declarative
- precise
- institutional
- quiet urgency

**Function:**
- open the encounter
- establish Measures Registry tone register
- orient the visitor before Temple branching
- no intake
- no diagnosis
- no commerce

**Voice:**
> AI is not the authority. Systems must become governable.

**Tone must not:**
- claim authority
- diagnose visitor state
- imply urgency beyond quiet recognition
- introduce commerce or assessment signals

## Tone Boundary

Chamber tone is a governed layer.

Tone does not authorize:
- runtime implementation
- CSS modification
- audio implementation
- DB mutation
- payment
- subscription
- email
- social automation

Tone governs how a chamber sounds and feels in the contract layer.

Implementation of tone through audio, media, or CSS waits for its respective OAR2.
