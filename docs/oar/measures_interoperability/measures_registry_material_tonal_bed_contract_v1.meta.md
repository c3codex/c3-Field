# Measures Registry — Material Tonal Bed Contract v1

## Contract Purpose

The Material Tonal Bed Contract defines the tonal bed assigned to each material place in the Measures Registry installation: its audio function, the chamber state it carries, and what it must not do.

Material tonal beds carry chamber atmosphere. They do not author truth.

No tonal bed may imply authority, recognition, payment, conversion, or Commerced Circuit activation.

Tonal beds support encounter state only.

## Tonal Bed Rule

Material tonal beds carry chamber state.

They support recognition, passage, assessment, continuity, and governed transition.

They do not replace contract logic.

They do not override DB-governed encounter content.

## Tonal Bed Definitions

### 1. Signal Threshold Bed

**Assigned to:** Epigraph Place

**Tone register:** quiet, opening, institutional

| Audio Function | Constraint |
|---|---|
| Quiet opening pulse — establishes encounter presence | Must not assert authority |
| Institutional gravity — signals that this is a governed system | Must not create emotional urgency |
| Threshold marking — distinguishes pre-encounter from encounter | Must not imply assessment or commerce |
| Breath / space — allows the visitor to arrive before the encounter begins | Must not crowd the opening moment |

**Bed must not:**
- claim authority over visitor state
- introduce fear, urgency, or commercial pressure
- imply diagnosis before Lapis orientation

## Tonal Bed Boundary

Material tonal beds are a governed contract layer.

Tonal beds do not authorize:
- audio implementation
- media upload or bucket work
- CSS modification
- runtime implementation
- DB mutation
- payment, subscription, email, social, or support automation

Tonal bed implementation through audio, media, or CSS waits for its respective OAR2.
