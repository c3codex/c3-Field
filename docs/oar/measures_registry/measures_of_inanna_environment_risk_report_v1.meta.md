# Measures of Inanna — Environment Risk Report

Governed discovery evidence for OAR2 ROUTED sections 7 and 16. Read-only. No recommendation below is authorized action; all require operator decision.

## 7. Release, access, visibility, and permission review

Standings kept independent per OAR2 instruction (not treated as synonyms): `release_state` (registry) vs. `release_state`/`access_state` (explicit `measures_release_state`) vs. `access_state` (registry) vs. runtime `access_state` (`c3_runtime_admission_contract`/`binding`) vs. public semantic `pairing_state` (`c3_public_semantic_pairing`).

- **`gate_4_breastplate` — registry/release-state conflict (active defect, confirmed).** `measures_registry.release_state = held`, `access_state = gated`. `measures_release_state.release_state = released`, `access_state = gated`, with `release_reason: held_by_phase_map` / `access_reason: gated_by_phase_map` — i.e. the explicit release-state row says "released" while its own reason text and the parent registry row both say "held." The two tables disagree on the single most consequential field (`release_state`) for the same unit. Evidence: `measures_registry` row + `measures_release_state` row, both read 2026-07-15, joined on `registry_id`.
- **`crystal_temple_home`, `temple_antechamber`, `temple_harrumuk_passage` — access_state label mismatch (semantic drift, lower severity).** All three show `access_state: visible` in `measures_registry` but `access_state: callable` in `measures_release_state`. `release_state` agrees (`released`) in all three. Could be intentional (parent-row "visible" vs. explicit-standing "callable" as different axes), but the OAR2 explicitly forbids treating these as synonyms without evidence they are meant to differ — no such distinguishing evidence was found. Flagged as **semantic drift requiring resolution**, not silently reconciled.
- **`temple_antechamber_return` — missing explicit release-state row.** Registry and encounter-def rows exist and are active; no corresponding `measures_release_state` row was returned. **Missing evidence**, not assumed held or released.
- **Public standing vs. intended held standing:** no row was found where a *held* registry parent has a *publicly readable* encounter definition (the encounter_def rows for held units — Gates 4–7, Epithets 4–9, MEs 02–13 — are all themselves `is_active: true` at the row level, which is expected: the row exists and is DB-readable to an authorized query, but `c3_chamber_directory_binding`/`c3_runtime_admission_binding` gate actual public runtime exposure, and no binding row was found admitting any of these held units to public runtime). No conflict found here.
- **Public semantic pairing — all 6 non-guard pairings are `held`, 2 are `blocked`.** Every `c3_public_semantic_pairing` row for Measures Registry's public-facing vocabulary (`Assess the Environment`, `Governed Pathway`, `Measures Assessment Protocol`, `Measures AI Operational Evaluation`, `Structural Drift Detected`, `Understand the Environment`) is `pairing_state: held`, `public_use_allowed: false`. Two guard rows (blocking leakage of internal circuit codes `c1_c2_c3` and `direct_mapped_federated`) are `pairing_state: blocked` by design. This means the public vocabulary layer for the assessment/MAP flow is not yet live anywhere, independent of the encounter-release standing reviewed above — a second, separate gate on top of encounter release.
