# OAR2 — Encounter Boundary Native Architecture Discovery

## OBJECTIVE

Establish Encounter Boundary as the native architectural transition between
prepared state and encounterable state.

Deprecate Encounter Shell as an independent architectural layer.

Boundary becomes the sole presentation threshold.

---

## OBSERVED

Previous architecture assumed that runtime or shell would somehow infer
intended arrangement from Codex-held relationships.

This assumption has proven false.

Codex can hold.

Systems can align.

Measures can determine.

Field can arrange.

None of these alone produce encounter.

An encounter only exists when every required component has been intentionally
placed, contained, and held into a single bounded state.

Only then may encounter occur.

---

## DISCOVERY

Field does not create encounter.

Field creates arranged potential.

Encounter Boundary confirms that arranged potential is complete.

Renderer does not determine.

Renderer manifests.

The renderer never discovers intention.

It only manifests an encounter already made possible.

---

Who encounters.

---

## ENCOUNTER BOUNDARY

Encounter Boundary is not a software shell.

Encounter Boundary is the constitutional threshold where prepared state
becomes encounterable state.

Above the boundary:

- authority
- registry
- release
- determination
- arrangement
- composition

Below the boundary:

- manifestation
- presentation
- interaction
- observation

Nothing below the boundary may determine standing.

Nothing above the boundary may present encounter.

---

## DEPRECATION

Retire:

- Encounter Shell

Reason:

Shell duplicates responsibilities already inherent to Encounter Boundary.

Boundary naturally hosts:

- lifecycle
- presentation frame
- callback plumbing
- unavailable state
- environment dispatch
- renderer handoff

No independent shell abstraction is required.

---

## CHAMBER RELATION

Chambers do not contain encounters.

Chambers contain the required condition families that make encounter possible.

Field arranges those condition families.

Encounter Boundary confirms readiness.

Renderer manifests.

---

## DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Existence is not arrangement.

Relationship is not encounter.

Naming is not behavior.

Every required component may exist.

Every relationship may exist.

Until intentionally placed, contained, and held within Encounter Boundary,
encounter remains only potential.

---

## CLAUDE ROLE

Claude may:

- replace shell references with Encounter Boundary where appropriate
- preserve renderer isolation
- preserve ChamberRouter dispatch
- preserve RenderableEncounter boundary
- maintain presentation-only behavior below Encounter Boundary

Claude may not:

- reintroduce shell abstraction
- move authority below Encounter Boundary
- move presentation above Encounter Boundary
- infer arrangement
- invent encounter state

---

## VALIDATION

Success is achieved when:

- Encounter Boundary becomes the sole presentation threshold.
- Shell no longer exists as an architectural concept.
- Chambers retain condition responsibility.
- Field retains arrangement responsibility.
- Renderer retains manifestation responsibility.
- Authority and presentation remain permanently separated.

---

## CLOSE

The Encounter Boundary is the first place where arranged potential becomes
encounterable.

It is not where intention is inferred.

It is where intention, having already been arranged, becomes capable of being
lived.

Nothing is invented.

Everything required is present.

Encounter may now occur.
