# OAR2 — Measures Registry Launch Flow + Assessment Chamber Alignment

## OBSERVED

Measures Registry launch flow has reached strong structural coherence:

intro
? landing (2 paths)

LEFT PATH
UNDERSTAND FAILURE
? recognition passage
? assessment explainer passage
? MEASURES AI ASSESSMENT chamber
? assessment completion
? talking-head structured environment passage
? structured environment

RIGHT PATH
BUILD COHERENCE
? direct structured environment / implementation pathway

The uninterrupted encounter flow is functioning as a primary strength.

Current drift points remain:

1. inconsistent button language
2. weak continuation language (`Skip`)
3. assessment explainer page feels spatially hollow
4. assessment chamber risks feeling like a generic form
5. evaluation currently lacks fully intentional chamber identity
6. assessment completion requires stronger branded transition into Structured Environment
7. soft SRC capture must occur before diagnostic progression
8. assessment must feel structurally governed while evaluating whether the institution’s AI environment is

The desired encounter condition is:

the environment itself feels coherent, governed, intentional, and structurally complete while the institution’s AI governance standing becomes the only unstable object under assessment.

Frontend remains encounter-side execution only and may not invent authority, routes, or unsupported DB state.

## ALIGNED

This OAR2 governs only the LEFT PATH:

UNDERSTAND FAILURE
? Assess the Environment

It does not govern the BUILD COHERENCE path.

The binary landing distinction must remain clear:

LEFT PATH
- recognition
- evaluation
- structural drift
- assessment progression

RIGHT PATH
- implementation
- governance structure
- coherence-building pathway
- direct institutional alignment

The launch flow should preserve:

- cinematic encounter continuity
- contained viewport progression
- no dead-end surfaces
- minimal navigation
- no SaaS dashboard feeling
- no popup-form energy
- no marketing clutter
- no generic onboarding patterns

The Assessment Chamber must feel:

- intentional
- branded
- governed
- spatially coherent
- structurally calm
- singular in purpose

The assessment itself becomes the central encounter action.

The user should feel:

- the environment is already structured
- their AI governance standing is what is being evaluated

The chamber is not:
- a lead form
- a survey
- a growth funnel
- a startup onboarding wizard

It is:
- a governed diagnostic chamber

## ROUTED

### 1. Uniform button language

Replace inconsistent utility labels sitewide.

Remove:
- Skip
- Sound

Use:
- Audio
- Continue

Context-specific continuation labels allowed:

- Continue to Evaluation
- Continue to Diagnostic
- Enter Structured Environment

Buttons remain:
- small
- restrained
- right-aligned
- visually uniform across the site

### 2. Assessment explainer passage

The assessment explainer passage must:

- auto-load the existing 45-second explainer video
- preserve cinematic encounter pacing
- enlarge video presence within viewport
- reduce hollow surrounding dead space
- provide visible support text for non-audio viewing
- preserve dark chamber atmosphere
- avoid header/footer clutter
- transition automatically into Assessment Chamber after completion
- allow manual continuation

Recommended visible copy:

Before evaluation, recognize the environment.

Most AI instability is not model failure alone.

It emerges where authority, validation,
oversight, implementation structure,
and behavioral registration are unclear
or absent.

This passage prepares the assessment chamber.

Primary action:

Continue to Assessment

### 3. Assessment Chamber identity

Assessment Chamber loads immediately after explainer completion.

The chamber must feel:

- intentionally structured
- branded
- governed
- spatially complete
- singular in purpose

The chamber should visually imply:

the only thing currently unstructured is the institution’s AI governance condition.

No alternate routes should dominate the chamber.

Avoid:
- external nav
- article distractions
- secondary CTA clutter
- generic form presentation
- oversized marketing copy

### 4. Chamber visual system

Assessment Chamber visual language:

- obsidian/lapis field foundation
- marble governance accents
- restrained crystalline geometry
- controlled spacing
- typography consistent with Measures Registry identity
- subtle marble tonal continuity if Audio enabled
- contained viewport-first composition

The assessment should feel:
- measured
- institutional
- architectural
- intentional

not:
- casual
- app-like
- startup-like
- survey-like

### 5. Chamber branding

Assessment Chamber title:

MEASURES AI ASSESSMENT

Opening chamber copy:

This chamber evaluates the structure surrounding your AI use.

Authority.
Validation.
Oversight.
Implementation.
Behavioral registration.

The system is structured.
The assessment identifies whether your AI environment is.

Primary action:

Begin Assessment

### 6. Soft SRC capture sequence

Before diagnostic progression:

Capture:
- Company / Organization Name
- Type of Business / Organization
- Contact Name
- Email

This is soft SRC intake alignment only.

Introductory copy:

Before the diagnostic begins,
identify the environment being assessed.

Continuation button:

Continue to Diagnostic

### 7. Evaluation chamber progression

Assessment questions should render inline within the chamber.

Avoid:
- popup forms
- embedded third-party survey appearance
- giant scrolling intake walls

Preferred behavior:

- controlled progression
- chamber-state transitions
- one diagnostic grouping visible at a time
- clear continuation logic
- visible progression standing

The user should feel:
- they are entering data into a governed system
- not completing generic paperwork

### 8. Assessment completion state

Completion page branding:

MEASURES AI ASSESSMENT COMPLETE

Supporting text:

Structural conditions have been recorded.

Continue into the Structured Environment.

Primary action:

Enter Structured Environment

This completion surface should:
- feel resolved
- remain restrained
- preserve chamber continuity
- transition directly into the talking-head structured environment passage

### 9. Structured Environment passage

After assessment completion:

- load uploaded R2 talking-head video
- preserve marble tonal continuity
- maintain restrained cinematic pacing
- provide visible support text if audio unavailable
- transition directly into Structured Environment

Primary continuation:

Enter Structured Environment
