# OAR2 - Register Nine Roles and role_call Standing Before FREE Repair

## PURPOSE

Register the native nine-role structure and role_call standing before repairing FREE.

Chambers assemble.

Roles authorize.

Passages move.

Renderers manifest.

Optics proves.

Nothing is invented beyond the nine roles defined in this OAR.

## TERMINOLOGY STANDING

The term contract is reserved for smart_contract only.

Legacy implementation fields using contract language must be classified as legacy implementation fields and mapped to native standing.

Do not create new contract terminology.

Use:

- agreement
- resolution
- boundary
- requirement
- role_call
- passage
- smart_contract only when referring to actual smart contracts

## NATIVE ROLE LIMIT

There are only nine roles.

Roles exist within three material families:

- Obsidian
- Lapis
- Marble

Each material family contains three roles.

3 materials x 3 roles = 9 roles.

No role may be invented outside these nine.

No role may be duplicated.

No chamber owns a role.

A chamber may call Obsidian, Lapis, or Marble roles.

## NINE ROLES TO REGISTER

### Obsidian roles

1. Obsidian Gatekeeper

Authority:
- constraints
- eligibility
- safe entry
- gate conditions

Function:
Authorizes whether an encounter may enter, continue, or remain held.

2. Obsidian Examiner

Authority:
- assessment
- evaluation
- condition tracing
- exposure detection

Function:
Authorizes assessment mechanics and evaluation passage.

3. Obsidian Witness

Authority:
- recorded encounter evidence
- boundary acknowledgment
- submission standing
- trace preservation

Function:
Authorizes what is recorded as encounter evidence.

### Lapis roles

4. Lapis Guide

Authority:
- relational passage
- orientation
- human-touch movement
- participant-facing guidance

Function:
Authorizes guided movement between encounter states.

5. Lapis Scribe

Authority:
- visible language
- publication copy
- dispatch language
- public wording

Function:
Authorizes copy, text, publication surfaces, and dispatch language.

6. Lapis Steward

Authority:
- integration handling
- contact capture
- secure passage
- communication handoff

Function:
Authorizes secure participant handoff and contact passage.

### Marble roles

7. Marble Resolver

Authority:
- findings
- result standing
- resolution
- carried-forward outcome

Function:
Authorizes result state and what may be carried forward.

8. Marble Cartographer

Authority:
- MAP routing
- pathway mapping
- next-step structure
- transition destination

Function:
Authorizes mapped pathway and next encounter direction.

9. Marble Sealkeeper

Authority:
- registry standing
- Codexstone relationship
- held or active state
- standing confirmation

Function:
Authorizes whether standing is held, active, registered, or sealed.

## role_call STANDING

A role_call is the act of bringing a role into encounter.

role_call determines:

- what passages may occur
- what agreements may be encountered
- what resolutions may be carried
- what media may be revealed
- what transitions may be authorized
- what copy may be public
- what submission may be recorded
- what completion state may be reached

If role_call is absent:

- copy may fall back
- transitions may drift
- passages may disappear
- media may lose context
- encounters may render without authority

The system may still operate.

The system does not fully align.

## CHAMBER STANDING

A chamber assembles.

A chamber may call:

- Obsidian roles
- Lapis roles
- Marble roles

A chamber does not own the roles it calls.

A chamber may not invent a role.

A chamber may not bypass role_call when the encounter requires role authorization.

## FREE REPAIR IMPLICATION

FREE must not render by chamber assignment alone.

Required order:

EncounterBoundary
  -> role_call
  -> Chamber assembles
  -> Role authorizes
  -> Passage moves
  -> Renderer manifests
  -> Optics proves

FREE currently renders by chamber assignment but does not yet fully resolve role_call.

This OAR registers role_call standing only.

Do not repair FREE renderer behavior in this OAR.
