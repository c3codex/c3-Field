# OAR2 — Restore Crawlable Institutional Content Authority

## OBSERVED

AI visibility authority has been restored through JSON-LD, llms.txt, semantic headings, crawlable navigation, internal linking, and public identity signals.

The site still remains semantically thin from a crawler perspective.

Measures Registry's ideas are present visually and experientially but remain underexposed in crawlable HTML.

This is now a content visibility problem rather than a schema problem.

## ALIGNED

Measures Registry is not a local business website.

Measures Registry is an institutional governance and education platform.

The solution is not SEO filler pages.

The solution is a crawlable institutional semantic map that accurately expresses what Measures Registry is, what problems it addresses, what pathways exist, and how concepts relate.

Frontend may expose already seated meaning.

Frontend may not invent authority.

Codex remains authority.

## ROUTED

### 1. Establish Public Institutional Authority Pages

Priority order:

1. About Measures Registry
2. AI Isn't Broken. Systems Are.
3. Structural Drift
4. Governable Environments
5. Measures Alignment Protocol
6. Assess the Environment
7. Our Story
8. Foundational Leadership
9. unDrifted

If routes already exist, strengthen content.

If routes do not exist, do not invent them unless they are approved and seated.

Pages must explain institutional concepts.

Pages must not expose pricing, payment, certification claims, DAO standing, c3 Key standing, or conversion claims.

### 2. Increase Crawlable Homepage Authority

Homepage should include 300 to 700 words of crawlable institutional copy explaining:

- Measures Registry provides institutional governance for AI deployment.
- Structural drift is measurable.
- AI outcomes emerge from the systems in which AI operates.
- Governable environments can be designed and maintained.
- The public assessment is a baseline awareness tool.
- MAP is a later governed pathway.

Copy should remain integrated with encounter architecture.

No generic SaaS sections.

No keyword stuffing.

### 3. Establish Topic Clusters

Cluster A:

- AI Governance
- Governable Environments
- Institutional Accountability

Cluster B:

- Structural Drift
- System Integrity
- Operational Outcomes

Cluster C:

- Measures Alignment Protocol
- Assessment
- Governed Optimization

Cluster D:

- Foundational Leadership
- Our Story
- unDrifted

### 4. Expand Internal Linking Graph

Every institutional page should link to About Measures Registry, Structural Drift, Assess the Environment, and unDrifted where contextually appropriate.

Internal links must use real anchor href attributes.

Do not use JS-only navigation for crawlable internal links.

The site should become traversable through HTML alone.

### 5. Add Article Schema

Where appropriate, add Article, NewsArticle, or BlogPosting schema for unDrifted articles, public educational articles, and authored publications.

Schema should include:

- headline
- author
- publisher
- datePublished
- dateModified
- description

No invented publication dates.

No invented authors.

### 6. Add Founder Authority Surface

Create or strengthen founder bio, authorship section, and organization/person relationship.

Public-safe details only:

- Stephanie Joanne Gaffney
- artist
- Measures Registry Instructor
- founder of c3 Community Partners DAO, LLC

No private information.

### 7. Improve Citation Readiness

Public pages should answer:

- What is Measures Registry?
- What is Structural Drift?
- What is a Governable Environment?
- What is MAP?
- Why do AI outcomes depend on system structure?

Pages should be understandable without video playback.

### 8. Preserve Encounter Architecture

Institutional content additions must not replace encounter flow, create generic SaaS layouts, break visual design, or bypass registry rendering principles.

Semantic authority should support the encounter, not replace it.

## VALIDATION

This OAR resolves successfully when:

- homepage contains substantial crawlable institutional text
- public authority pages form a semantic map
- internal linking graph expands
- article schema exists where appropriate
- founder authority is visible
- AI systems can understand Measures Registry without requiring JS execution or video playback

## CODY ROLE

Cody may add crawlable institutional copy, create approved public pages, strengthen semantic structure, implement article schema, improve internal linking, and improve citation readiness.

Cody may not invent authority, create pricing claims, create certification claims, expose private standing, or replace encounter architecture with generic marketing pages.
