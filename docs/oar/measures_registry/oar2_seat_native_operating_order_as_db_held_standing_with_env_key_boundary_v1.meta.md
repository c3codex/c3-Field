## ENV_KEY BOUNDARY

`env_key` must be seated as future secured completion boundary.

Standing:

- `env_key` is not required for public FREE launch surfaces unless a surface is secured
- `env_key` is required for finished secured Measures Registry environment continuity
- `env_key` does not replace public route standing
- `env_key` does not replace role authorization
- `env_key` does not replace Measures release/access standing
- `env_key` supports secured retrieval, continuity, correction, and scale passage

Passages and antechambers remain held for secured/scale until `env_key` boundary is implemented.
