# OAR2 - Seat Public Legal Surfaces Before Launch

## GOVERNANCE STANDING

Governance belongs to the body.
Agency belongs to the individual.
Integrity belongs to both.

This OAR governs required public legal surfaces before launch.

It does not govern the operator.

Purpose is to seat launch-safe legal surfaces and verify encounter placement.

Nothing is invented.
Nothing is assumed.
Evidence precedes mutation.

## OBSERVED

Measures Registry is approaching public launch.

Assessment pathways collect contact information.

Email communication pathways exist.

MAP pathways exist.

Public legal surfaces are required.

Codex holds.

- informational purpose
- no guarantees

- prohibited use

- changes to service

- request access

- request deletion
- communication preferences
- contact pathway

- support boundaries

- opt-in state

No dark patterns.
