# OAR2 — Seat unDrifted Publication Brand and Style Contract v1

## OBSERVED

Measures Registry has active governed landing-page standing for:

- `/ai-operations-assessment`
- `/structural-drift`

The Structural Drift publication relation is active and DB-backed.

Current publication standing includes:

- published publication registry row: `structural_drift`
- published dispatch count: `2`
- visible published dispatch content including `Agents of Chaos`

Deprecated eval passage residue has been audited and corrected.

Current public SEO route standing is governed and crawler-visible route metadata remains present.

The next active seam is publication identity and style.

Current risk:

- `Structural Drift` is compelling as a diagnostic series name
- but as a publication umbrella it can position Measures Registry around the failure condition rather than the governed correction
- common terms such as `Integrity System` and `System Integrity Report` are already occupied by ethics/compliance and cybersecurity meanings
- Measures Registry requires a publication brand that is native, memorable, non-oppositional, and not borrowed from compliance or cyber-audit vocabulary

Operator has selected:

- publication brand: `unDrifted`

The generated visual direction established a usable target:

- dark editorial interface
- obsidian/graphite field
- restrained lapis/cyan signal
- fracture-aware structural texture
- publication dispatch layout
- Measures Registry parent authority
- article/series cards
- no compliance beige
- no alarmist AI panic
- no cyber dashboard clutter

This OAR2 seats the unDrifted publication brand and style contract so future article/dispatch work can render consistently from governed state.

## ALIGNED

Authority order remains:

Codex -> Field -> Measures -> OAR2 -> Chazz -> Cody -> src

unDrifted is a Measures Registry publication/dispatch surface.

unDrifted does not replace Measures Registry authority.

unDrifted does not define conversion, certification, payment, wallet, c3 Key, SRC, DAO, permission, recognition, distribution, or Marble readiness.

unDrifted is an education/orientation publication brand.

Structural Drift becomes a diagnostic series within unDrifted.

Articles/dispatches must be registered before renderer exposure.

Frontend may render the publication style, but may not own:

- publication identity
- article truth
- dispatch order
- release state
- CTA target
- claim boundary
- metadata standing
- social preview standing

The generated visual target is accepted as design reference only.

The governed style contract is the implementation authority.
