# OAR2 - Perceptual Hierarchy + Material Spaciousness Addendum v1

## OBSERVED

The Runtime Coherence Optics chamber successfully evolved into relational governance geometry through:

- fracture visibility
- radial relation mapping
- continuity vectors
- correction arcs
- rooted field structure

However, the emergence of relation density introduced a new condition:

informational density began competing with perceptual spaciousness

Earlier Crystal-layer optics preserved:

- stillness
- breathable spatial relation
- centered resonance
- ceremonial encounter
- silence around authority

The newer Lapis geometry correctly exposes relation and continuity, but risks flattening all layers into equal visual urgency.

Current standing therefore reveals an unresolved optics balance between:

informational relation

and:

perceptual encounter

## ALIGNED

True c3 optics requires:

hierarchical perceptual emergence

The chamber should not expose all information equally or simultaneously.

The chamber must allow:

- coherence to be felt before analyzed
- authority to remain spacious
- relation to emerge progressively
- fracture to become perceptible through tension
- inscription to appear as preserved continuity
- lineage to reveal itself through encounter depth

The runtime chamber is not:

- analytics software
- total-information exposure
- flattened governance reporting

The runtime chamber is:

a living perceptual governance field

Crystal should merge into the field, not remain visually separate from it.

- source authority

silence around authority

- threshold
- refusal

- tense

Marble is not isolated to a single region.

distributed continuity memory across the Field

4. Marble inscription continuity discovered fourth
