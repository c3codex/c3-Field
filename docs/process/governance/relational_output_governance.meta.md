# Relational Output Governance

## Purpose

Seat the distinction between:

- authority determination
- relational rendering
- institutional expression

So AI may adapt encounter expression without mutating Codex authority.

---

## Core Rule

AI determines relational output.

AI does not determine authority.

---

## Native Order

Codex → authority  
Field → relation  
Measures → conditions + registration  
Chazz/AI → relational rendering  
src → encounter presentation

---

## Relational Output Definition

Relational output is the encounter-facing expression generated from seated state.

Examples:

- contextual phrasing
- institutional clarification
- threshold framing
- tone adaptation
- educational explanation
- temporal orientation
- passage conditioning

Relational output may vary.

Authority may not.

---

## Authority Boundary

AI may NOT determine:

- truth
- recognition
- verification outcome
- schema meaning
- release authority
- registry standing
- role reassignment
- bypass conditions

If state is not seated:

- AI may clarify absence
- AI may not invent resolution

---

## Passage Rule

Internal passage semantics determine encounter context.

They do not automatically render as public copy.

Example:

internal:
recognition_passage

public:
Before Evaluation
Recognize the Environment

Passage semantics govern:

- threshold tone
- routing posture
- encounter conditioning
- relational framing

---

## Institutional Identity Rule

Institutions remain distinct in identity while conforming to shared authority conditions.

AI may adapt:

- tone
- explanatory language
- contextual framing
- institutional relationality

AI may not alter:

- authority structure
- verification requirements
- Measures conditions
- Codex standing

---

## Monitoring Owner

NotChazz monitors boundary collapse before render acceptance.

OAR2 routes correction when a boundary flag is raised.

---

## NotChazz Monitoring

### Flag Types

- INTERNAL_CONTEXT_RENDERED_AS_COPY
- UNFRAMED_SCHEMA_EXPOSURE
- AUTHORITY_RELATION_COLLAPSE
- AI_AUTHORITY_SUBSTITUTION
- UNGOVERNED_RELATIONAL_OUTPUT
- MISSING_RELATIONAL_FRAMING

---

## Runtime Validation Rule

Before render acceptance:

1. verify seated authority state
2. verify relational context
3. verify public framing
4. verify authority not mutated by AI output
5. verify schema/public distinction preserved

---

## Success Condition

The system is coherent when:

- institutions remain recognizable as themselves
- authority remains invariant
- AI adapts relation without mutating truth
- schema governs context
- public language clarifies encounter
- NotChazz flags semantic collapse before acceptance

Codex holds.  
Field structures.  
Measures registers.  
Chazz renders relation.  
NotChazz preserves distinction.
