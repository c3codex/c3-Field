## Proposal

Plateau Commons is a place-based rural Tennessee proposal in which residents, workers, artists, educators, small businesses, and local institutions define the priorities, acceptable uses, benefit conditions, and boundaries for AI in their communities.

c3 Field supplies structured participation and relational infrastructure. Plateau Commons is the place-based commons and demonstration environment. Measures Registry remains the bounded institutional readiness and implementation boundary when institutional work is required.

The objective is not AI adoption for its own sake. The objective is community agency: people most affected by technological and economic change set the terms before tools, institutions, or funders define the intervention.

## Community-led rule

People most affected by technological and economic change define the goals, terms, priorities, acceptable uses, and benefit conditions. Experts, institutions, funders, and AI systems may support the work but do not substitute for community authorship.

## Working scope

1. Establish a community-defined Current State baseline through structured participation.
2. Surface resident, worker, artist, educator, small-business, and local-institution priorities before selecting AI interventions.
3. Define locally authored AI use conditions, unacceptable uses, benefit expectations, and decision pathways.
4. Prototype bounded community-selected applications and economic opportunities without making AI adoption the objective.
5. Retain evidence of what the community defined, what was tried, what changed, and what remains unresolved.
6. Develop a replicable rural-community participation model from the place-based demonstration.

## Explicitly out of scope

- AI literacy as the primary outcome
- technology adoption for its own sake
- expert-led governance substituted for community authorship
- selling participant attention, data, relationships, or reach
