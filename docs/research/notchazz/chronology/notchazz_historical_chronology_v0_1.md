# Formation trajectory

The currently evidenced trajectory is:

1. **Named concept** — NotChazz exists as protected systems intelligence.
2. **Voiced correction** — it interrupts naming, source, cadence, state and authority drift.
3. **Flag mechanism** — specific structural distinctions are protected through named flags.
4. **Dedicated R&R** — incidents receive their own report/resolution surfaces.
5. **Boundary detector** — surface and role collapse become the recurring object of detection.
6. **Generalized validation** — named flag classes and pre-acceptance checks appear.
7. **Operational protection** — holds, constraints and refusal of false continuity enter the operating spine.
8. **Trigger/response relation** — specific conditions map to hard stops and Operator return.
9. **Environment specificity** — chamber/system/environment identity becomes part of applicability.
10. **Implementation** — a reusable system/environment guard is tested.
11. **Current consolidation** — c3Ops ownership, environment-scoped profiles, explicit trigger responses, evidence return, resolution lineage and reviewed learning proposals.

## Strongest retained relation across the chronology

Across the recovered sources, the most stable function is not `error detection` in the ordinary sense. It is:

**detect when a system is about to continue under a relation, authority, identity, role, state, or completion claim that the evidence does not support; refuse that false continuity; preserve the condition; return control/evidence to the proper authority path.**

This is a normalized research finding, not a rewrite of any historical source.
