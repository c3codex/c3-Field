# MRM Memory Governance Contract

MRM means registered relationship continuity. Its governance belongs to Memory-aligned governance, not to a chamber directory.

- Chamber directories may place encounters; they may not govern contacts, relationship continuity, signal truth, conversion, or certification.
- Contact/consent evidence may feed a future registered relationship trace only through a separately seated contract.
- Missing MRM persistence, lifecycle, or read-model proof remains `pending_verification`.
- This package creates no MRM table, record, credential, contact, conversion, or certification state.
