# Social Media Campaign Contract

The campaign contract packages approved source content for distribution without transferring content authority to a social platform.

- Source publication: unDrifted/Measures Registry governed article state.
- Distribution targets: X and Instagram are recorded; Facebook-specific authority is pending verification.
- Scheduler: Buffer package evidence exists, but submission requires capability and operator approval.
- Replacement: a new approved campaign supersedes an older campaign package; it does not rewrite publication truth.
- Prohibited: auto-post, credential creation, engagement automation, or inferred account standing.
