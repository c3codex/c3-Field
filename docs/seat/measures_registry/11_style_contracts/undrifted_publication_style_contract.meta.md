# unDrifted Publication Style Contract

unDrifted uses an institutional editorial and report-like visual hierarchy. A contact-consent action may render only when seated by the encounter/action contract.

This style contract does not activate C1 commerce, payment, c3 Key, Field access, Marble, MAP, SEAT registration, conversion, or certification. Paragraph and social surfaces remain integrations governed by their own contracts.
