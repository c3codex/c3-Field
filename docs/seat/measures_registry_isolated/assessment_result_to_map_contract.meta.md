# Assessment Result to MAP Contract

An assessment result is a bounded recommendation surface. It is not registration or certification.

The result may identify structural drift or an operational-environment need and may recommend or route toward MAP the Environment after consent. Consent and a recommendation do not themselves activate MAP, payment, or scheduling.

The result does not activate payment by itself.

The result also does not:

- activate SEAT;
- assign a c3 Key;
- grant Field access;
- confirm registration;
- certify a system.

Any runtime route from result to MAP remains held pending seated route authority and exact-row DB reconciliation.
