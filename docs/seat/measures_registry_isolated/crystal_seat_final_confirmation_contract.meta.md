# Crystal Seat Final Confirmation Contract

Crystal Seat is not an encounter and is not a chamber.

Crystal Seat is the final confirmation and registered-standing surface. It opens only after SEAT is confirmed and the system is registered through separately seated authority.

Crystal Seat does not open directly from:

- assessment;
- an assessment result;
- payment;
- MAP purchase;
- MAP execution without confirmed SEAT standing.

The active `crystal_chamber` encounter is a distinct chamber surface and must not govern Crystal Seat. No exact `crystal_seat` registry row was recovered through the prior public read.

c3 back office remains held unless separately authorized.
