# Scheduling Boundary Contract

Scheduling occurs only after confirmed payment or governed operator approval. Scheduling may open a live MAP review session after a separately seated scheduling contract is satisfied.

Scheduling does not activate SEAT, confirm registration, assign a c3 Key, grant Field access, or open Crystal Seat.

Scheduling must preserve the registered contact, consent, and MRM trace. The current MRM persistence and lifecycle authority remain incomplete, so scheduling activation is held.
