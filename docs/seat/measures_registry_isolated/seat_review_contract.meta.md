# SEAT Review Contract

SEAT review occurs after MAP deliverables and determines whether the system can be confirmed.

SEAT review is not:

- public assessment;
- MAP purchase;
- payment;
- registration by itself;
- Crystal Seat by itself.

When its requirements are satisfied through separately seated authority, SEAT review may prepare final confirmation standing and route to Crystal Seat only after SEAT is confirmed and the system is registered.

SEAT review remains held until separately seated.
