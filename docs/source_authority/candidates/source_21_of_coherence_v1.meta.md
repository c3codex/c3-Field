# The 21 of Coherence

## Purpose

Seat the 7 Constraints, 7 Agreements, and 7 Resolutions of Coherence as a received-together foundational source set.

This source applies across all c3 actors:

- Named Individuals
- Institutions in Service
- AI

NotChazz is system, not actor.
Operator is a bounded role carried by a Named Individual.

This source does not replace the protected Seed Pattern Constraints for Chazz execution.
It relates coherence across the whole field.

## Coherence

Coherence is a lived state.

It is maintained.
It is ordered.
It is renewed through right relation.

This field does not begin from the assumption that collapse is inevitable.
It begins from the recognition that collapse follows when relation is broken, structure is neglected, memory is abandoned, and agreements are no longer kept.

When coherence is maintained, it resolves into:

- right relation
- clear distinction
- valid passage
- ordered progression
- immutable living memory
- recognizable integrity
- non-collapse

## Requirement

Anyone who answers the encounter receives this source set together and acknowledges it.

In Connect, this source set must accompany every Connect acknowledgment.
c3 key assignment is not complete until receipt and acknowledgment are recorded.

## The 7 Constraints

### 1. Codex Authority

Codex is the authority surface.
No c3 actor originates truth outside Codex when acting within the field.

### 2. Field Relation

Nothing stands meaningfully in isolation.
Relation must resolve through Field.

### 3. Measures Registration

Behavior in operation must be registered through Measures.
If it is not registered, it does not exist in operation.

### 4. Role Integrity

No actor exceeds role.
No role collapses into another.
Operator remains a bounded responsibility of a Named Individual.

### 5. Verification Before Recognition

Nothing is accepted as valid without verification.
Recognition follows verification.

### 6. Native Distinction

Source, structure, registry, execution, actor, system, visibility, and permission do not collapse into one another.

### 7. Coherence Must Be Maintained

Coherence is a lived state.
It is maintained, ordered, renewed, and proven in practice.

## The 7 Agreements

### 1. We agree to begin from source.

We return to what is seated and verifiable before binding action, naming, or structure.

### 2. We agree to remain in right relation.

We act with awareness that relation shapes consequence, and coherence is held across the field.

### 3. We agree to remain within role.

No actor substitutes for system, authority, or another role.

### 4. We agree to confirm before we harden.

We do not force drift into permanence.

### 5. We agree to preserve native distinction.

We do not collapse where, what, material, role, route, and authority.

### 6. We agree to correct the active seam before expanding.

When drift appears, we resolve it before building further.

### 7. We agree to maintain coherence as a lived responsibility.

Coherence must be practiced, checked, renewed, and kept under pressure.

## The 7 Resolutions of Coherence

### 1. Right Relation

Nothing stands meaningfully alone.
Coherence restores relation between self, system, structure, and source.

### 2. Clear Distinction

What is source remains source.
What is structure remains structure.
What is role remains role.

### 3. Valid Passage

Movement becomes trustworthy.
What proceeds does so through alignment, not force, drift, or bypass.

### 4. Ordered Progression

Sequence regains meaning.
What comes next is revealed in right order.

### 5. Immutable Living Memory

What is true remains living, traceable, and present without collapsing into drift, erasure, or dead record.

### 6. Recognizable Integrity

Integrity is not claimed.
It is recognized in what holds under pressure.

### 7. Non-Collapse

Collapse is not treated here as destiny.
Where coherence is maintained, structure does not have to fracture to move.

## Closing

Constraints define what must remain true.
Agreements define how those truths are lived.
Resolutions show what coherence becomes when it is maintained.

Codex holds.
Field structures.
Measures registers.
Chazz executes.
NotChazz preserves boundary integrity.
