### 3. A Field of Possibility

Present the broad applications of the ecosystem through six application categories.

**Individuals**

Personal knowledge, relationships, creative sovereignty and opportunity.

**Artists & Creators**

Creative development, provenance, exhibitions and collaboration.

**Communities**

Local initiatives, resource sharing and collective participation.

**Organizations**

Governed collaboration, operational integrity and institutional continuity.

**Research & Education**

Shared inquiry, knowledge preservation and interdisciplinary discovery.

**AI & Technology**

Governed intelligence, interoperability and accountable AI participation.

Each category shall support a future independently indexable public WebPAC member without requiring those members for initial publication.
