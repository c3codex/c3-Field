## Append-only amendment v1.1 — Relationships, computational relations and environmental governance

**Relationships are encountered. Computational relations are qualified. Environments are governed.**

Nothing governs relationships themselves. Governance establishes and protects applicable conditions within environments, not the meaning, existence or direction of relationships. Connect does not compel relationship; Contribute does not compel participation; Create does not compel outcome. Coherence and relationship are not manufactured by the system. A relationship may exist independently of its recognition in the Registry. Absence of registered evidence does not establish absence of relationship.

The c3 Model expresses relational structure without claiming authority over relationships. References above to governing relations mean invariant conditions of environmental governance and qualified computational relations, never governance of relationships themselves.

### Registry responsibility
Computational relations are essential to coherent registered state. Establish, connect and verify exact bindings among identities, environments, PACs, NUGs, sources, capabilities, permissions, ownership, custody and evidence as applicable. Validate direction, scope, provenance, receiving conditions, effective standing, withdrawal and succession. A computational relation represents only what its qualified evidence supports; it does not confer authority merely by existing or capture the full meaning of a relationship. Do not infer consent, ownership, intimacy, obligation or permission from proximity, interaction or a graph edge.

SRC reads environmental conditions and qualifies applicable computational relations through the 3x3 grid, including formation and succession. NotChazz evaluates boundaries; Registry persists and enforces qualified state; CURRENT reflects qualified relational evidence without defining the underlying relationship; FREE resolves effective registered bindings. Preserve native environmental responsibility rather than compulsory Registrar mediation. NUG availability and invocation require their own applicable qualification; neither relationship nor registered relation alone grants effects.

### On-soil expression
A land registry can recognize documented ownership without defining someone's relationship with the land. An environment may establish conditions for access, contribution and stewardship without compelling attachment, participation or creation. Consent, rights, observable evidence and applicable law remain distinct. The same distinction applies across computational and on-soil expressions.

Demonstrate that an encounter without a registered relation does not manufacture a binding or deny the existence of a relationship; that valid exact bindings resolve without granting unintended authority; that absent, ambiguous, stale or withdrawn bindings refuse dependent effects without purporting to terminate underlying relationships; and that formation/succession preserve identity, ownership, custody and evidence without compelled participation. Verify independent receiving-environment qualification and privacy. Distinguish voluntary relational evidence from inferred meaning, and registered rights from personal relationships. Use existing native mechanisms and the same 3x3 grid; no new relationship authority, compulsory registration, independent ledger or global relationship score.
