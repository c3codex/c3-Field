# c3 Model

## Purpose

The c3 model defines the non-extractive process architecture of the field.

Its purposes are:

1. define process architecture  
2. define value movement  
3. define intrinsic OAR logic  

No additional purposes are assumed unless introduced through the canon decision protocol.

---

## Model Definition

The c3 model is the process architecture through which participation and value move coherently across the field.

It is expressed through three circuits:

- **Connect**
- **Contribute**
- **Create**

This model is coherence-based, non-extractive, and oriented toward commons restoration through structured participation rather than coercion or capture. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

The model is not a marketplace mechanism alone, nor a governance mechanism alone.

It is the motion logic that allows encounter, contribution, and creation to remain legible to one another.

---

## Circuit I — Connect

**Connect** governs encounter without coercion.

Its OAR articulation is:

- **Objective:** Orientation
- **Action:** Browse, read, view, listen
- **Result:** Recognition without coercion :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

In the broader c3 model, Connect allows works, writings, collections, and exhibitions to be encountered without price pressure, wallet requirements, token gating, or premature calls to convert. Orientation precedes valuation. Recognition precedes transaction. Trust is established before participation. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

Connect is therefore the opening circuit of coherence.

It initiates relation without forcing extraction.

---

## Circuit II — Contribute

**Contribute** governs voluntary value entry.

Its OAR articulation is:

- **Objective:** Allow voluntary valuation
- **Action:** Buyer chooses value at acquisition
- **Result:** Fair compensation and commons funding :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}

In this circuit, value enters the system transparently and non-extractively.

Creative work may be contributed without speculative pricing, and value may be chosen voluntarily at the moment of acquisition. The value split is fixed and transparent, with one-third to artist or creator, one-third to initiative operations, and one-third to commons treasury. 

Contribute is the circuit where value becomes legible as relation rather than domination.

---

## Circuit III — Create

**Create** governs shared manifestation.

Its OAR articulation is:

- **Objective:** Restore shared infrastructure
- **Action:** Allocate commons funds via governance
- **Result:** On-soil stabilization and continuity :contentReference[oaicite:8]{index=8}

In this circuit, accumulated commons value is translated into real-world structures such as land, cultural spaces, educational and healing environments, architectural or sound-based infrastructure, and other restoration pathways. Governance acts here as stewardship of allocation, not curation of meaning. 

Create is the circuit through which potential becomes durable commons form.

---

## OAR as Intrinsic Logic

The c3 model does not merely contain OAR.

It moves through OAR.

Within the model, OAR operates in two inseparable readings:

### 1. Objective · Action · Result

This is the circuit articulation of c3.

It clarifies what each circuit is for, what action occurs within it, and what result the circuit is designed to produce. Existing c3 materials already define Connect, Contribute, and Create in this form. 

### 2. Observed · Aligned · Routed

This is the coherence trace of c3.

It records:

1. what was observed  
2. what it aligns to  
3. where it routes next :contentReference[oaicite:11]{index=11}

Observed · Aligned · Routed is append-only governance-grade motion logic. It is not a feed and not a diary. It preserves coherent movement through the system over time. :contentReference[oaicite:12]{index=12}

Together, these two readings make OAR both the articulated logic and the trace logic of the c3 model.

Without Objective · Action · Result, the model loses clarity.

Without Observed · Aligned · Routed, the model loses memory.

Without both, the model becomes stagnant.

## Relation to Codexstone, DAO, and CoherentAI

The c3 model operates inside the Codexstone–DAO container and is supported by CoherentAI as an embedded coherence function across the field. Codexstone provides structural environment and recognition surface, the DAO provides governance and stewardship continuity, and CoherentAI supports coherent intercorrelation without becoming governance itself. :contentReference[oaicite:13]{index=13}

The model is therefore process architecture, not container, not pillar, and not external tool.

It is the way participation moves inside the field.

## Relation to Measures and Priceless Gallery

Measures of Inanna demonstrates the c3 model through descent, recognition, and governance across Gates, Epithets, and MEs, functioning as a complete demonstration of Connect, Contribute, and Create. :contentReference[oaicite:14]{index=14}

Priceless Gallery functions as proof-of-application, demonstrating open encounter, voluntary valuation, and commons benefit in public-facing cultural form. 

These applications do not replace the model.

They make it visible.

---

## Model Rule

The c3 model shall remain non-extractive.

It must preserve:

- open encounter without coercion
- voluntary value flow without speculative pressure
- commons creation without capture
- OAR clarity in articulation
- OAR memory in motion

Any implementation of c3 that removes OAR from the model removes the model’s capacity to remain coherent over time.

---

## Closing Principle

The c3 model is not static structure.

It is coherent motion.

Connect opens relation.  
Contribute modulates value.  
Create stabilizes shared form.

OAR gives this motion clarity, memory, and route.

Without OAR, c3 does not circulate.  
It stalls.
