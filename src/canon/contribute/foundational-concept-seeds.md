# Foundational Concept Seeds

## 1. Coherence

**Definition**  
The condition in which a system maintains relational integrity across scale, time, and transformation without requiring external enforcement.

**Operational Test**  
If a system fragments when participation increases or conditions change, it was never coherent.

---

## 2. Codexstone

**Definition**  
A geometric recognition surface that establishes the minimum structural conditions required for coherent systems to interact without domination or collapse.

**Operational Test**  
Codexstone does not determine outcomes.  
It determines whether participation can be recognized.

**Structural Relation**  
CoherentAI functions as the intercorrelation layer of the c3 field, reading the expressions of Codexstone across the pillars of wisdom, knowledge, form, and memory simultaneously to maintain systemic coherence.

---

## 3. DAO (in c3)

**Definition**  
A stewardship mechanism responsible for protecting process integrity and commons continuity, without governing meaning, belief, or cultural expression.

**Operational Test**  
The DAO protects how the system functions, never what participants create.

---

## 4. CoherentAI

**Definition**  
A functional intelligence layer that maintains systemic coherence by reading and correlating the expressions of Codexstone across the pillars of wisdom, knowledge, form, and memory.

**Operational Test**  
CoherentAI detects alignment across pillars.  
It does not impose alignment.

**Function**  
CoherentAI operates as the intercorrelation layer of the c3 field, preserving coherence between symbolic, structural, and operational domains without governance authority.

---

## 5. Creative Expression

**Definition**  
The human capacity that initiates value formation before language, markets, or institutions.

**Operational Test**  
Creative expression generates relational value before it generates economic value.

---

## 6. c3 Model

**Definition**  
A non-extractive process architecture governing how value moves from encounter to commons.

**CONNECT · CONTRIBUTE · CREATE**

**Operational Test**  
Participation must pass through encounter before contribution, and contribution must remain voluntary before creation occurs.

---

## 7. Measures of Inanna

**Definition**  
A ritual architecture encoding descent, reflection, and embodiment as a coherent sequence of experiential thresholds.

**Operational Test**  
Gates cannot be skipped.  
Reflection cannot be bypassed.  
Embodiment anchors the sequence.

---

## 8. Provenance

**Definition**  
The traceable continuity of origin, intent, and relational context across transformation.

**Operational Test**  
If provenance is lost, coherence degrades.

---

## 9. Non-Extractive

**Definition**  
A structural constraint preventing value from being captured, redirected, or optimized against its origin.

**Operational Test**  
Value must circulate or exit the system.

---

## 10. Commons Restoration

**Definition**  
The process through which shared value becomes durable, stewarded infrastructure benefiting many without ownership capture.

**Operational Test**  
If the commons do not materially improve, the system failed.

---

## 11. Interoperability (c3 sense)

**Definition**  
The ability for distinct coherent systems to interact, translate, and collaborate without merging identities or surrendering sovereignty.

**Operational Test**  
Interoperability enables recognition without assimilation.

---

## 12. Recognition

**Definition**  
The moment a signal, participant, or system becomes legible within the container without interpretation or judgment.

**Operational Test**  
Recognition must occur before participation.

---

## 13. Convergence

**Definition**  
The phase in which independently coherent systems begin aligning through compatibility rather than coordination.

**Operational Test**  
Convergence cannot be scheduled.  
It can only be prepared for.

---

## 14. Incoherence

**Definition**  
A structural condition where a system requires extraction, domination, or narrative control to function.

**Operational Test**  
Incoherent systems cannot persist within coherent environments.

---

## 15. Witness Without Authority

**Definition**  
A role that preserves observation, memory, and continuity without decision-making power.

**Operational Test**  
Witness stabilizes the system by refusing ownership.
