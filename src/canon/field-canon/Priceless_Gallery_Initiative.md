# The c3 Model — Connect · Contribute · Create

A coherence-based system for cultural value, commons restoration, and on-soil action.

**c3 DAO — System Role**

The c3 DAO exists to steward commons value generated by aligned initiatives.  
It does not curate art, set prices, or dictate creative direction.

The Priceless Gallery is the first official initiative operating within this model.

---

## Circuit 1 — Connect

**Encounter without coercion**

**Objective:** Enable open encounter with creative work and initiatives without price pressure, obligation, or identity gating.

**Action:**
- Public access to artworks, collections, writings, and exhibitions
- No price tags
- No token requirements
- No calls to buy, join, or convert

**Result:**
- Orientation precedes valuation
- Recognition precedes transaction
- Trust is established before participation

**System Constraints:**
- No pricing metadata displayed
- No algorithmic prioritization based on sales
- No DAO interaction at this stage

**Smart-contract implication:** CONNECT is read-only. No state changes. No wallet required.

---

## Circuit 2 — Contribute

**Value enters the system**

**Objective:** Allow artists and participants to contribute creative work or value into a transparent, non-extractive system.

**Action — Artist side:**
- Artist submits work under an initiative
- Work is registered with attribution, provenance, and circulation permissions
- No pricing set by artist or platform

**Action — Buyer side:**
- Buyer chooses a value at the moment of acquisition
- Buyer initiates a transaction voluntarily
- Value is split automatically

**Fixed Value Split (encoded rule — immutable):**
- 33% → Artist
- 33% → Initiative operations (The Priceless Gallery)
- 33% → Commons Treasury (c3 DAO)
- 1% optional buffer for platform or transaction fees

**Result:**
- Artists are compensated fairly
- Initiatives sustain themselves
- Commons value accumulates transparently
- No speculative pricing dynamics

**System Constraints:**
- No minimums
- No suggested prices
- No negotiation
- Split is immutable

**Smart-contract implication:** CONTRIBUTE is state-changing. Funds routed deterministically 33/33/33. DAO does not receive funds until this circuit completes.

---

## Circuit 3 — Create

**Commons value becomes real-world structure**

**Objective:** Translate accumulated commons value into on-soil initiatives that restore stability, access, and shared infrastructure.

**Action — Commons funds may be allocated to:**
- Land acquisition
- Cultural spaces
- Exhibitions
- Educational and healing structures
- Architectural or sound-based environments
- Community stabilization projects

**Selection Pathways (signal → authorization):**

1. Special Exhibitions — signal cultural relevance, measure participation and contribution
2. Rising Star Contributors — demonstrated alignment and responsibility; capacity precedes authority
3. DAO Voting — final authorization only; allocates commons funds; no content curation, only stewardship

**Result:**
- Art directly funds lived conditions
- Commons are restored materially
- Creative intelligence becomes infrastructure
- The system sustains future creation

**System Constraints:**
- DAO cannot curate art
- DAO cannot set prices
- DAO cannot override initiative boundaries
- Voting applies only to commons allocation

**Smart-contract implication:** CREATE is governance execution. DAO proposals restricted to funding amount, scope, and timeline. Outcomes are logged and auditable.

---

## The Priceless Gallery — First Initiative of the c3 DAO

**Role within the model:**
- Operates fully across all three circuits
- Serves as the proof-of-function initiative

**In practice:**
- CONNECT: open gallery, no prices
- CONTRIBUTE: buyer-chosen value, fixed split
- CREATE: commons funds routed to DAO for on-soil initiatives

**Why it matters:**
- Demonstrates the c3 model end-to-end
- Establishes trust through structure
- Creates a template for future initiatives

---

## Summary

The c3 Model operates through three circuits — Connect, Contribute, and Create — ensuring that creative work is encountered freely, valued voluntarily, and translated into shared infrastructure. The Priceless Gallery is the first initiative within the c3 DAO, enabling artists to contribute work without pricing pressure, buyers to choose value at acquisition, and commons funds to be stewarded transparently through DAO governance. This structure restores circulation, protects creative sovereignty, and converts cultural value into real-world stability.
