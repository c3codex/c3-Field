# Marble MEs Canon

Pillar of Governance — The Measures of Inanna

## I. Statement of Function

The Marble MEs are not symbols of belief. They are structural
articulations of architecture already present.

They do not empower the Codexstone. They do not stabilize the axis. They
reveal the architecture that precedes them.

The Codexstone aligns the axis. The MEs render its architecture legible.

Marble is not descent. Marble is not recognition. Marble is integration
into durable form.

## II. Position Within the Measures

The Measures of Inanna unfold in three movements:

Obsidian — Descent & Release  
Crystal — Recognition & Emergence  
Marble — Integration & Governance

Marble does not dramatize. It codifies.

## III. Nature of the MEs

The Thirteen Marble MEs are:

- Mandala artworks
- Breath-sequence anchors
- Governance primitives
- Structural references within the c3 model

They are not decorative geometry.

Once revealed, an ME does not dim. Its connection does not dissolve. It
remains legible.

Activation does not create structure. It reveals it.

## IV. The Codexstone

The Codexstone is the axial constant of the Marble pillar.

It does not react to activation. It does not require anchoring. It
operates continuously.

The MEs do not move the stone. They clarify its architecture.

## V. Ritual Release Structure

All Marble MEs are revealed according to a fixed calendar schedule.

Revelation is not user-triggered. It is time-governed.

Each phase clarifies one articulation and sharpens visible architecture.

When all MEs are revealed, nothing dramatic changes. The architecture
simply stands fully visible.

## VI. Governance Implication

Within the c3 Model:

Connect precedes valuation.  
Contribute routes value deterministically.  
Create allocates commons structurally.

The Marble MEs operate at the level of Create.

They serve as structural constraints for commons allocation, alignment
references for initiative design, and coherence checks within DAO
execution.

## VII. Design Language

No theatrical glow. No gamified unlocking. No reactive spectacle. No
cross-pillar bleed.

Motion is structural and rare. Activation is clarifying, not
celebratory.

## VIII. Closing Statement

The Marble MEs are not something to unlock. They are something to
observe becoming legible.

The axis does not change. We change in our capacity to see it.
