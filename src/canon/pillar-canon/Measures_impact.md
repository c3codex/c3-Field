# Measures of Inanna — An Exhibition in Three Movements

Measures of Inanna is the work.

Descent, Epithets, and Mandala Expressions are its measures.

Each measure is distinct in purpose. Each applies a different form of pressure. Each reveals coherence in its own way.

Inanna is present within each measure as Measure itself.

She does not depend on recognition.  
She does not require interpretation.  
She is not altered by passage.

---

## The Three Movements

**In Obsidian Gates, the measure is descent.**  
What cannot remain coherent is released.

**In Crystal Epithets, the measure is reflection.**  
Once ego has descended, true forms can surface.

**In Marble MEs, the measure is embodiment.**  
What is whole becomes stable.

---

## Form of Encounter

Each measure is encountered with original artwork, motion, sound, and fragments drawn from early Sumerian source material.

Nothing here asks to be understood.  
Nothing here seeks agreement.

The measure holds with or without witness.
