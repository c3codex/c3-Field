## What the Priceless Gallery Is

The Priceless Gallery is the **first official initiative operating within the c3 DAO model** and the proof-of-function expression of the full Connect → Contribute → Create circuit.

It is the east-facing pillar of Codexstone — the encounter-without-demand surface where creative work is visible, valued voluntarily, and translated into commons infrastructure.

It is not a marketplace.  
It is not a portfolio aggregator.  
It is a governed encounter surface where orientation precedes valuation, recognition precedes transaction, and trust is established before participation.

---

## Three-Circuit Structure

The Priceless Gallery operates fully across all three c3 circuits.

### Circuit 1 — Connect
Open encounter with no price pressure, no token requirements, no calls to buy or convert.

- Public access to artworks, collections, writings, and exhibitions
- No pricing metadata displayed
- No algorithmic prioritization based on sales
- Read-only — no state changes, no wallet required

### Circuit 2 — Contribute
Value enters the system voluntarily through a fixed, immutable split.

- Artist submits work with attribution, provenance, and circulation permissions
- Buyer chooses value at moment of acquisition — no minimums, no suggested prices
- Value split is encoded and immutable:
  - **33%** → Artist
  - **33%** → Initiative operations (Priceless Gallery)
  - **33%** → Commons Treasury (c3 DAO)
  - *(1% optional buffer for platform/transaction fees)*

### Circuit 3 — Create
Accumulated commons value is translated into on-soil initiatives.

- Commons funds may be allocated to land, cultural spaces, exhibitions, healing structures, community stabilization
- Selection pathways: Special Exhibitions → Rising Star Contributors → DAO Voting
- DAO voting applies only to commons allocation — never to content curation or pricing

---

## Canonical Constraints

The following are binding constraints that may not be overridden by implementation decisions:

1. **The value split is immutable.** 33/33/33 is encoded — no negotiation, no override.
2. **The DAO does not curate art.** It stewards commons value only.
3. **The DAO does not set prices.** Buyer-chosen value is the only pricing mechanism.
4. **Connect is read-only.** No state changes, no wallet required at the encounter layer.
5. **Works are registered canonical objects.** Attribution, provenance, and circulation permissions are registry-driven — not hardcoded.
6. **DAO voting scope is restricted.** Proposals are limited to funding amount, scope, and timeline.

---

## Pillar Boundaries

The Priceless Gallery is **interoperable** with the other three pillars but **not interchangeable** with them.

It may:
- inherit shared field canon
- surface within the Codexstone container
- route commons value to the Restore pillar via the DAO
- reference Measures or c3 Model contexts without merging into them

It may not:
- govern installation architecture (that belongs to Measures)
- govern DAO commons allocation rules (those belong to Restore)
- collapse its encounter model into a transactional or promotional pattern
