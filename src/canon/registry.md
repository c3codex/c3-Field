# Canon Registry

## Purpose

This registry defines the **13 field-level canon documents** of the c3 environment.

The registry functions as:

1. the official canon list  
2. the ordering surface for field canon  
3. the inheritance root for pillar canon and operational systems  

---

# Canonical Principle

The **Codexstone** is the coherent container.  
The **c3 model** is the living circuit within that container.

Field canon defines what must remain true.  
Pillar canon inherits from field canon.  
Operational systems implement those truths without redefining them.
