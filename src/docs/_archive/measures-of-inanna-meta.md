## Purpose

This document is the **pillar-level canon meta** for Measures of Inanna.

It defines:

- what Measures is
- what it governs
- how it inherits from field canon
- how its encounter units are structured
- what cannot be changed without a formal canon challenge

---

## What Measures Is

Measures of Inanna is a **contained ritual and cultural architecture** rendered as a living installation.

It is not a gallery. It is not a content site.  
It is a structured descent, recognition, and integration sequence — built from canonical symbolic forms and rendered as encounter units from a governed data layer.

The installation is composed of three symbolic layers:

- **Seven Obsidian Gates** — Threshold, release, shedding, passage. The descent sequence.
- **Nine Crystal Epithets** — Reclaimed qualities, restored naming, visible return. The recognition sequence.
- **Thirteen Marble MEs** — Stable governance forms, breath anchors, principle-bearing structures. The integration sequence.

---

## Structural Logic

Measures inherits from field canon under the **Create** chamber.

The symbolic movement triad is:

```
Descent → Recognition → Integration
```

Mapped to the deep structural triad:

```
Source → Passage → Form
Codexstone → Gates → MEs
```

Measures is the canonical implementation of this triad in living installation form.

---

## Canonical Constraints

The following are binding constraints that may not be overridden by implementation decisions:

1. **Gate sequence is immutable once sealed.** Gates may not be reordered, renamed, or removed after sealing.
2. **Epithets belong to the recognition layer only.** They may not be used as navigation elements or decorative labels.
3. **MEs are governance-bearing forms.** Any system task touching an ME must produce a guard event and an OAR log.
4. **Media resolves through `measures_media` and `v_measures_media_resolved`.** Pasted URLs and local registries are forbidden.
5. **The temple is sealed.** The installation surface is rendered from canonical state. It does not author truth.

---

## Pillar Boundaries

Measures is **interoperable** with the other three pillars but **not interchangeable** with them.

It may:
- inherit shared field canon
- reference the c3 model circuit
- surface within the Codexstone container

It may not:
- merge its gate or ME structure with gallery or restore logic
- have its encounter units resolved by components acting as source-of-truth resolvers
- be governed by any authority layer below pillar-level canon
