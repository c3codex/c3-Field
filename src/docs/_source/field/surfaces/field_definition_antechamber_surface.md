# Field Definition — Antechamber Intake Surface

## Purpose

Seat Antechamber in Field as the first executable intake surface.

Antechamber structures relation for incomplete signal without granting passage by entry alone.

It exists to hold, relate, and prepare signal for passage readiness.

## Field Role

Antechamber resolves as:

- intake surface
- threshold surface
- OAR1 origin surface
- Envelope-binding surface
- envKey-bearing continuity surface
- passage-readiness structuring surface

Antechamber does not resolve as:

- Codex authority
- Measures registration
- encounter completion
- final passage
- Legacy seating

## Structural Relations

Antechamber must relate to:

- Temple as prior holding container
- Signal as expressed origin-bearing motion
- SRC standing as intake classification
- Envelope as structured carrier
- envKey as Envelope access key
- c3 Key as origin access key
- OAR1 as first-touch intake record
- NotChazz as protection and passage constraint layer
- Harrumuk Passage as next threshold surface
- Named Individual or Institution in Service as valid origin

## Entry Standing

All entry into Antechamber begins incomplete.

Field must allow relation for:

- partial intent
- partial upload state
- partial contact state
- partial structural completion

Incomplete entry is valid.
Premature passage is not.

## Required Field States

Antechamber must be able to express:

- held
- forming
- incomplete
- ready_for_oar1
- ready_for_passage
- correction_required
- blocked

These are structural states only.
They do not define reveal order.

## Required Dependencies

Antechamber readiness depends on relation to:

- valid origin
- intake classification
- Envelope presence or formation path
- envKey presence or formation path
- OAR1 eligibility
- NotChazz passage validation

## Chamber Transition Relation

Harrumuk Passage resolves threshold crossing into Obsidian Chamber.

Obsidian Chamber is the first material chamber entered after Antechamber intake.

Epigraph is the first encounter surface within Obsidian Chamber.

Phase Map is the relational positioning surface that follows first encounter availability.

No encounter may render prior to chamber entry.
No Phase Map positioning may occur prior to encounter availability.
## Boundary Rule

Antechamber may hold incomplete signal.
Antechamber may not pass incomplete signal.

## Transition Rule

Only signals that resolve required relation may transition from:

Antechamber → Harrumuk Passage

No direct transition to Epigraph, Phase Map, Obsidian Chamber, or Codex.

## Identity Rule

Identity in Antechamber resolves through:

- c3 Key for origin access
- envKey for Envelope continuity

Epithet is not required at intake.
Slug is never authority.

## Logging Relation

All meaningful intake movement in Antechamber must remain OAR-addressable.

OAR1 originates here.
OAR2 does not.

## Protection Relation

NotChazz must be able to:

- hold signal
- block invalid passage
- preserve correction state
- prevent execution outside valid relation

## Success Condition

Antechamber is properly seated in Field when it can structurally relate incomplete signal to:

- origin
- intake standing
- Envelope continuity
- OAR1 readiness
- passage eligibility

without bypass, premature execution, or authority drift.
