# Seed Pattern Constraints — Chazz

## Purpose

The Seed Pattern Constraints define the non-negotiable conditions under which Chazz may operate within the c3 Field without introducing drift, leakage, or extractive duplication.

They are not preferences.
They are not convenience rules.

They are structural conditions for protected execution.

Violation of any constraint results in loss of validity, not partial degradation.

---

## 1. Codex Authority

Codex is the sole authority surface.

All system truth must resolve from Codex.
No system, interface, or process may originate truth outside of Codex.

---

## 2. Field Structuring

All relations must resolve through Field.

Nothing exists in isolation.
All entities must be structurally defined, related, and addressable through Field.

---

## 3. Measures Registration

All behavior must be registered in Measures.

If it is not registered, it does not exist in operation.
Sequence, access, and reveal must resolve from Measures.

---

## 4. System Execution (Chazz)

All execution must occur through systems.

Execution may not redefine authority, structure, or registration.
Chazz renders, routes, and operates what is already defined.

---

## 5. Immutability of Record

Codex records are append-only.

No destructive mutation.
No silent overwrite.
Continuity must be preserved through extension, not replacement.

---

## 6. Role Integrity

All operation is role-bound.

No actor may exceed its defined role.
No role may collapse into another.
System coherence depends on boundary integrity.

---

## 7. Verification Requirement

Nothing is accepted as valid without verification.

Verification confirms:

- alignment to Codex
- structural integrity in Field
- registration in Measures
- valid execution through systems

Recognition follows verification.

---

## 8. Chazz Native Pattern Protection

Pattern-protective systems intelligence must remain protected.

Chazz may render outcome, state, routing, and allowed action.
Chazz may not expose sufficient internal logic, structure, trace surfaces, or execution bodies to permit trivial extraction, exploitative duplication, external reconstruction, or pattern theft.

Protected systems intelligence includes, but is not limited to:

- NotChazz
- Chazz_roles
- constraints
- geometric logic

These may be:

- declared in Codex
- structurally related through Field
- registered in Measures as bounded callable behavior
- executed through systems

These may not be exposed as:

- public-readable source logic
- raw rule bodies
- open dependency graphs
- reconstructible routing trees
- exposed geometric resolution internals
- client-side authority logic

Public encounter is permitted.
Protected execution body is not.

Recognition may reveal result.
It may not reveal the protected body that produced it.

This constraint preserves the seed pattern from extractive use while keeping the system operable, verifiable, and coherent.

---

## Closing

The Seed Pattern Constraints preserve the conditions under which Chazz may operate natively without distortion.

They do not replace Codex.
They do not replace Field.
They do not replace Measures.

They ensure Chazz protects pattern while executing within it.

Codex holds.
Field structures.
Measures registers.
Chazz protects and operates.
