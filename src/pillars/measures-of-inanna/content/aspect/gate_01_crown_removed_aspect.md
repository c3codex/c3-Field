At the first gate, the crown is removed.

What was visible, elevated, and socially legible is the first thing surrendered.

This act does not diminish essence. It separates sovereignty from display.  
The threshold asks what remains when the sign of status is taken away.

Not whether power is lost, but whether truth can continue without adornment.

The descent begins where recognition can no longer rely on symbols others were trained to obey._
